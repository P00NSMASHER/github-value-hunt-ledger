# COMBINATIONS

Integrator-owned active product stacks. This file is intentionally **not** a historical archive: Git history and hunter catalogs preserve prior variants. Keep only combinations that are materially more valuable than their components alone, solve a named buyer problem, and have a falsifiable validation path. Unknown, contradictory or insufficient evidence remains REVIEW/UNKNOWN and never becomes a positive claim or dollar amount.

## Portfolio invariants
- Separate **source/authority truth**, **deterministic expected state**, **actual observed state**, **evidence sufficiency**, **human/operational action**, and **realized outcome**.
- A component never validates itself; use independent comparators, hand-authored goldens, negative controls or later outcome evidence where possible.
- Public-repository commercial permission applies to repository-owned code/content under the user's standing assertion, not automatically to third-party standards, data, vendor services, customer records, patents, trademarks or bundled assets.
- No credential/private/confidential/accidental-exposure material enters a commercial stack.

## Combination promotion rule
A new stack enters this file only when it:
1. adds a capability missing from a current leader or creates a clearly better product shape;
2. preserves independent truth/evidence boundaries;
3. has a named buyer and smallest paid wedge;
4. has a cheap falsifiable stage gate in `EXPERIMENTS.md` or an explicitly recorded reason why it is not yet ready.

Do not create parallel combinations for every repository family. Fold challengers into the strongest existing stack and let experiments decide whether they deserve elevation.
