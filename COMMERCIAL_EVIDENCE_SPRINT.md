# Commercial Evidence Sprint Policy

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Phase:** 3 / Step 6  
**Purpose:** stop architecture growth from outrunning verified buyer and outcome evidence.

## Default rule

Net-new generic architecture is frozen until external commercial evidence improves.

Hunter, Technology Intelligence, Freight, RecoveryWorks and AI Business OS may continue work that:

1. closes a named evidence/data gap;
2. fixes a release/security/correctness defect;
3. supports an already-defined buyer experiment;
4. measures delivery cost, customer response or realized value; or
5. is required to keep an existing production path operational.

The following are not sufficient reasons by themselves:

- adding another generic agent/router/learner;
- adding another recovery vertical without buyer evidence;
- collecting repositories without a named product/evidence gap;
- increasing capability counts, test counts or architecture layers as an end metric.

## Commercial focus during the sprint

Primary external validation tracks:

- **FreightRecovery** — free audit -> authorized recovery engagement -> measured recovered funds / delivery cost.
- **CaptureBrief** — bounded buyer cohort -> paid or explicit no-value outcome -> measured delivery economics.

RecoveryOS is shared recovery infrastructure, not a third acquisition priority.

RecoveryWorks lanes remain available capabilities, but new lane development requires a buyer-linked evidence packet.

## Hunter search authorization

Broad discovery is paused as a portfolio objective. A Hunter run is authorized when its run packet names:

- target product/business;
- evidence gap or delivery blocker;
- expected decision changed by the result;
- stop condition;
- provenance/rights requirements.

## Exit criteria

The freeze can be reconsidered after at least one primary commercial track records a verified external outcome through the revenue loop, including explicit negative/no-value outcomes.

This policy does not authorize outreach, spending, customer commitments, production changes or money movement.
