# RETALLY Group operating model

Status: owner-approved internal planning direction; proposed organizational overlay.
Evidence snapshot: October 20, 2026. This document does not activate a public brand,
change legal ownership, or establish commercial readiness.

## Authority and scope

`ai_business_os/` remains the canonical implementation and **`ai_business_os_prod`
remains authoritative for private live operating state**. The public catalog and
dashboard are a read-only organizational preview. Source-to-canonical identity
mappings require reconciliation against that private system before integration.
Catalog groupings cannot merge businesses, customers, repositories, permissions,
or accounting records. Existing source identities and evidence remain intact.

The customer-register schema is an empty specification with executable shape and
relationship validation. The [private observation adapter](RETALLY_GROUP_PRIVATE_INTEGRATION.md)
can inspect existing business records and source counts through an authenticated
private runtime. Customer-register input remains optional and separately supplied.
No customer store, workflow mutation, cross-product access, or hosted customer
portal is created by this overlay. Public files contain source references and
planning metadata, never customer documents or confidential engagement records.

The [source reconciliation record](RETALLY_GROUP_SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md) now
distinguishes private business identities, public project identities, product
families, research sources and original private artifacts. It recovers the three
previously missing product sources and retains the separate ScopeSignal/CaptureBrief
association conflict. Source references and public project registration do not
supply private business bindings.

## Four proposed divisions

| Division | Offers and projects | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RETALLY Recovery | Freight, AP, warranty/supplier credits, procurement, rebates, parcel, utilities; remaining RecoveryWorks branches as expansion coverage | Document evidence-backed financial discrepancies and support explicitly authorized recovery. Branch code or synthetic acceptance does not establish real customer performance. |
| RETALLY Intelligence | CaptureBrief, PermitPlate, RehabSignal with original private offer documentation | Specialized research for distinct buyers. Each offer keeps its own qualification, evidence, delivery and authorization requirements. |
| RETALLY Tools & Data | SheetHarbor, x402 product family, App Factory draft local candidates with pinned source | Useful spreadsheets, software and data services. Product variants are not counted as separate companies. |
| RETALLY Ventures | ScopeSignal concept, Contractor Quote Recovery experiment, market-surveillance research, Resonance | Validate defined customer problems and deliverables before promotion. Research and demonstrations do not establish commercial readiness. |

**CaptureBrief** is a supervised Decision Evidence review of one existing public
federal IT/cyber/cloud pursuit, covering up to five buyer assumptions and a targeted
14-day watch. Its documented founding offer is $149 once, with no automatic renewal.
It does not provide broad opportunity discovery, proposal writing or bid submission.

**PermitPlate** provides founder-curated, source-linked NYC restaurant signals to
restaurant vendors. Its documented offer is $79/month for a weekly brief with up
to ten matching signals. It does not provide construction permitting consultancy
or guarantee opening dates, purchase intent or leads.

**SheetHarbor** comprises maker economics spreadsheets; its documented Excel
compatibility gate remains unresolved. **x402** is one product family with separate
production-reference and staging records; present availability and net revenue
need fresh evidence. **RehabSignal** has an original Pennsylvania SNF territory-brief
offer and execution plan; current delivery economics and demand need revalidation.
**App Factory products** have three implemented engines and nine default editions
on a draft, unmerged candidate branch; their [source map](RETALLY_APP_FACTORY_SOURCE_MAP.md)
does not establish a release. **Contractor Quote Recovery** is documented as a
historical outreach experiment without an established paid offer or dedicated
verified fulfillment system in its original audit. **Resonance** is senior-living cultural engagement planning;
demo evidence does not establish participant benefit. **Market surveillance**
remains research without trade execution. Personal/family projects, retired
products and unrelated private projects are excluded from commercial reporting.

## Shared responsibilities

The portfolio owner sets priorities and accepts commercial changes. Product-owner,
fulfillment, quality-review, finance-review and engineering responsibilities must
be assigned to actual authorized operators when an engagement exists; labels do
not invent staff or independent approval.

Portfolio Brain coordinates authorized engineering, reliability and reporting.
RecoveryOS supplies recovery evidence, calculations and case history. AI Business
OS supplies canonical operating governance. The Software Factory manages governed
engineering work; it does not prove its product candidates are sellable.

Keep the existing commercial-focus policy authoritative. The overlay proposes no
new allocation or automatic promotion. Add shared features when verified demand
from active offers establishes a concrete need.

## Customer workflow specification

The stages below describe the proposed private engagement workflow. They are not
implemented state transitions or permission grants in this preview.

| Stage | Evidence required before entry |
| --- | --- |
| Inquiry | Recorded request origin, received time, requested offer and a private source reference. Operator tests remain labeled separately. |
| Qualified | Offer-specific buyer fit, permitted input types, scope suitability and duplicate review. Similar names alone cannot merge customers. |
| Scoped | Customer-accepted service, deliverable, price model, responsibilities, time expectations and scoped data-use permissions. |
| Ready for fulfillment | Current service release/operating acceptance, required source evidence, assigned reviewer, exact service authorization and any contractually required advance-payment verification. |
| In fulfillment | Authorized task started with source-linked work records, artifact versions and appropriate private access. |
| Quality reviewed | Required service checks and an authorized review receipt bound to the exact deliverable version. Genuine independent review remains necessary wherever the service requires it. |
| Delivered | Exact reviewed artifact sent under specific send authority through an approved channel, with delivery/provider evidence. A generated file alone is insufficient. |
| Closed | Contractual completion or explicit cancellation disposition, remaining corrections/refunds resolved, and financial disposition recorded without inventing payment. |

Record failed checks and unresolved evidence as holds. Material source or artifact
changes require the relevant review to be repeated before further action.

**Payment status is orthogonal to delivery stage.** Advance-paid research and
contingency recovery have different timing. Maintain explicit payment, refund,
contingent and unresolved states with private supporting evidence. A payment does
not authorize delivery, a delivery does not prove payment, and customer recovery
does not itself establish RETALLY's fee revenue.

Each service needs its own scope and authorization. Prior purchase, common ownership,
an account match or shared brand implies no cross-selling, document-sharing,
counterparty-contact or funds-movement permission.

## Management measures

Unknown metrics remain `null` and display as unknown. Distinguish verified leads,
signed engagements, reviewed deliveries, collected fees, refunds, delivery costs,
operator effort and customer recoveries. Keep currency totals separate; never
silently convert currencies or double-count internal referrals. Private finance
evidence and the existing outcome-verification process govern recognized results.
Repository activity, simulations and project counts are not commercial outcomes.

## Milestone backlog

These are planning windows, not new scheduled tasks or promised completion dates.

| Window | Accountable responsibility | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 14 days | Portfolio owner + engineering | Reconcile canonical identities; resolve missing source bindings; retain exclusions; document readiness gaps and one next milestone per offer. Validate the read-only catalog, workflow specification and preview. |
| Through day 45 | Product owner + fulfillment + finance/quality reviewers | Prepare reviewed private-system integration; preserve current commercial priorities; establish offer-specific acceptance, effort and delivery records when genuine authorized engagements exist. Source changes require separate implementation and release review. |
| Through day 90 | Portfolio owner + product/finance reviewers | Assess actual demand, delivery effort and collected fees; propose relevant referrals only with specific permission; promote, revise or retain experiments based on evidence. Integrate shared workflows only where measured savings justify them. |

## Owner boundaries

Registration remains deferred under the owner's existing policy until an actual
customer. This is an operating proposal, not legal or tax advice. No new spending,
external contacts, added/rescheduled tasks or public rebrand activation are included.
The owner's “Get started” authorizes this source-only implementation; genuine
review, protected-branch controls and separate release requirements still apply.
