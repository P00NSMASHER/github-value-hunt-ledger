GROUP WEBSITE

Privacy at
RETALLY Group.

This notice covers the RETALLY Group directory website and general enquiries. Each operating business explains its own product, payment and engagement data practices on its website.

When you browse

The site presents business information and links. It does not provide a customer account, document upload or checkout. Hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to serve and protect the site.

When you contact the group

Email links open your email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. Your email provider and the receiving business mailbox process the message. Please include only the contact details and general context needed to route your enquiry; leave out confidential records, credentials and payment information.

Business-specific requests

For an existing order, payment, research request or recovery engagement, contact the responsible business. General group contact does not authorize another business to access your private records or begin work. Agree a suitable transfer route before sharing documents.

Retention and requests

Enquiry records are kept as needed to handle the request and meet applicable engagement or recordkeeping requirements. To ask about information you sent, request a correction or request deletion, email the group contact and identify the relevant enquiry. A deletion request may require checking obligations affecting email copies, backups or an ongoing engagement.
