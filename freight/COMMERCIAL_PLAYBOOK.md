## Plain-language service copy

**Find freight billing errors you can verify.**

We compare an agreed set of freight invoices with your rates and shipment records. You receive a reviewed discrepancy report with the source documents and calculations behind each supported finding, plus a clear list of missing information.

Your team decides which findings to pursue. We track later credits and refunds separately from discrepancies identified in the report.

**First step:** choose the $5,000 Readiness Review when records are uncertain, or the $15,000 Base Freight Audit when the base-scope records are ready.

Do not add guaranteed savings, customer logos, another recovery percentage, production-security claims or a turnaround starting before complete inputs and authorized kickoff. The standard optional managed-recovery split is 80% to the customer / 20% to Freight Recovery on fee-eligible net realized amounts only.

## Standard checkout path

1. The buyer selects the exact fixed service and pays once through the verified Stripe-hosted checkout.
2. Freight sends the receipt, plain-language terms, written scope confirmation and approved secure intake route. The buyer does not send freight records through checkout or ordinary email.
3. If the base scope cannot be accepted or a valid intake route cannot be provided, refund the fixed fee before work begins. The buyer may also cancel for a full fixed-fee refund before written scope confirmation.
4. The delivery clock begins after complete inputs and authorized kickoff, not on the payment date.
5. The buyer receives the reviewed deliverable and decides what happens next.
6. Managed recovery is optional and separately authorized after supported findings exist. No carrier contact, dispute or money-moving action is implied by checkout.

Stripe configuration must match the site: one-time USD prices of $5,000 and $15,000; no adjustable quantity, custom amount, subscription, card surcharge or automatic add-on. Any applicable tax must be disclosed before payment. Extra scope requires a separate written price and buyer approval.

## Qualification conversation: approximately 20 minutes

Record business metadata before requesting confidential records.

| Question | Decision informed |
|---|---|
| What billing/review problem prompted this discussion? | Buyer problem and useful acceptance criterion |
| Who owns freight costs, invoice review and this purchasing decision? | Economic buyer and operational owner |
| Which mode, carriers, business unit, currency and period can we review first? | Bounded population |
| How many invoices and source systems are involved? | Intake and review labor |
| Can you obtain controlling rates, amendments and shipment evidence? | Readiness and missing authority |
| How are existing audit findings recorded; can the output stay sealed initially? | Blind-comparison feasibility |
| How are prior/automatic credits and later refunds recorded? | Recovery attribution |
| What makes a report worth buying if no recoverable error is found? | Assurance/time-saving value versus recovery-only demand |
| What budget and decision date apply to a defined review? | Actual purchasing process and willingness to pay |
| What existing provider agreements/data restrictions must be considered? | Scope/access compatibility; buyer supplies relevant terms |
| Who reviews disagreements and how much time can they commit? | Buyer workload and turnaround |
| Which dated offer have you already received from us? | Preserve sample/contingency/fixed-fee commitments |

Disposition: **qualified next step**, **missing input with owner/date**, **future fit**, or **declined with reason**. Do not fill unanswered questions with estimates. Interest is not a sale; the historical prospect universe is not a qualified pipeline.

Existing tracker records have one-touch statuses. Any future separately authorized outreach must reconcile those states and recent responses first; do not use this playbook to restart contact or send another message automatically.

## Standard Base Freight Audit scope

The $15,000 checkout price covers this envelope:

- One buyer entity/business unit, one supported domestic mode and one currency.
- Up to **250 invoices**, **two carriers**, within **90 consecutive historical days**.
- Up to **three selected check families**, such as agreed base rate, duplicate billing or a supported accessorial rule. Confirm source authority, calculation support and reviewer competence for each.
- One agreed source bundle, frozen population and sealed incumbent submission.
- One report, one review meeting and one consolidated correction cycle.
- Additional sources/systems, later monitoring, carrier disputes and recovery administration are separate scope unless expressly included.
- Missing-source remediation and later settlement observation have agreed owners and deadlines.

If the requested work exceeds this envelope, do not auto-bill or quietly narrow it. Refund before work begins or offer a separate written scope and price through the controlled catalog process. A small population does not create an automatic discount. The buyer must accept the value and operative terms.

For an existing **20-invoice sample proposal**, preserve its offered terms. Freeze the selection rule, report excluded/missing records, and label the work a feasibility review rather than a representative estimate of annual losses. Customer files still require the valid controlled route.

| Readiness | Delivery lead + control owner | Current route decision, data requests and agreed Charter | Missing required launch evidence; payment does not override it |
| Freeze | Analyst + buyer records owner | Reconciled counts, selection rule and exclusions | Silent row changes, missing source or unresolved identity |
| Calculate | Analyst + qualified reviewer | Expected charges, source references and REVIEW decisions | Unsupported rule, wrong authority period or contradiction |
| Report | Reviewer | Complete dispositions, packets and separate totals | Unsupported dollars, inconsistent counts or unreviewed finding |
| Decide | Buyer approver | Accepted/disputed findings and exact actions | General consent substituted for a specific action approval |

| Learn | Commercial lead | Actual cost/hours, acceptance/payment and minimized outcome record | Proposed or synthetic amount presented as real evidence |

Review every asserted finding. Prespecify independent clean-record sampling before final results; disclose denominator and limitations. REVIEW records still count in workload and coverage even when asserted dollars are zero. Use the existing report/protocol instead of inventing a competing report vocabulary.

## Monthly monitoring: sell after the pilot earns it

Define source cadence, cut-off, check families, included volume, exception-review capacity, report date and buyer response obligations. Report missing/delayed sources explicitly; a failed feed is not a clean month.

Each recurring report contains:

1. Source/population coverage and unresolved records.
2. Reviewed new discrepancies and statuses.
3. Outstanding approved cases and later credits/refunds, net of observed reversals.
4. Repeated error causes and customer-approved corrections.
5. Review effort/cost against the declared baseline.
6. Scope/volume, service issues, fees and customer decisions.

A carrier/rate/source change triggers explicit coverage review. Select a recurring price from measured work; the $3,000/month scenario requires its own scope and agreement. Do not double-count corrected future charges, recovered historical amounts and time savings.

## One referral-partner experiment

Start after one direct paid engagement yields an accepted report. The partner should name the customer problem, provide a qualified introduction and disclose competing commitments.

Agree exact attribution and preexisting-account exclusions; a direct Freight/customer contract initially; commission base/duration/collection and refund handling; separate payment for delivery work; buyer-approved record access; and named decision owners. A proposed **20% of collected fixed fees for up to 12 months** is an internal negotiating hypothesis, not a sent offer.

Measure contribution after commission, sales effort and delivery costs. Do not justify a loss-making channel solely with assumed lifetime value. No partner may bind a buyer or approve its own disputed findings without the proper independent authority.

## Staffing and founder time

Set the founder's available hours before accepting deadlines; six hours/week is a planning assumption, not proven feasibility.

| Role | Initial owner | Capacity signal |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial decisions | Founder | Qualified selling/decision work exceeds available hours |
| Freight review | Named experienced practitioner with confirmed availability | Committed review exceeds reserved capacity |
| Delivery operations | Assigned per engagement; founder time costed if used | Intake/report/follow-up repeatedly displace selling or review |
| Technical operations | Owner of the actual environment | Support/change workload needs continuing paid coverage |

Use **80% of available paid delivery hours in the next four weeks** as an initial internal booking limit, leaving room for corrections and variability. This is a proposed rule, not an industry benchmark. Confirm cash and commitments before hiring; unsigned subscriptions are not payroll funding.

## Scorecard definitions

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Qualified opportunity | Named buyer/problem, input owner and explicit next step; one buyer cohort |
| Paid pilot | Accepted agreement plus evidence of the specified payment; unpaid contracts tracked separately |
| Proposal conversion | Paid pilots / decided qualified proposals in the same cohort/window; open proposals separate |
| Intake effort | All intake/support hours through a frozen usable population |
| Coverage | Every in-scope invoice assigned a reconciled disposition with reason |
| Finding precision | Confirmed findings / independently reviewed flagged findings, with counts and review basis |
| Missed problems | Errors in prespecified independent review of non-flagged records; sampling limits disclosed |
| Delivery margin | Fixed fee less loaded direct delivery costs, divided by fee; no potential recovery added |
| Channel contribution | Fee less delivery, commission and attributable acquisition costs |
| Recurring fees | Active contracted recurring base fees; billed/collected separate; one-time and variable fees excluded |
| Retention | Starting recurring customers retained / starting customers; new customers reported separately |
| Customer value | Distinct realized recovery, observed corrections and measured labor benefit without overlap |
| Concentration | Largest customer/partner share of actual recurring revenue, with denominator |

Small samples require counts alongside percentages. Existing pricing calibration needs five independently evidenced buyer cohorts; repeated engagements with one buyer do not satisfy it.

Current automated cohort summaries identify recorded stage co-occurrence, not proven chronological transitions or a mature conversion window. Store diagnostic, pilot and recurring start/payment dates and the observation cut-off before calculating timed conversion or retention. A later outcome appearing in the same buyer cohort does not by itself prove the earlier stage caused it.

## Decision checkpoints

These proposed rules guide spending; they do not automatically modify customer contracts:

- After ten qualified buyer conversations with purchasing decisions and no paid next step, revisit problem/segment/scope/input burden/price before adding features.
- After three qualified prospects fail on the same source requirement, repair qualification or acquisition of that source before promising delivery.
- After two pilots exceed the priced labor budget, pause new quotes at that scope until prospectively repriced or narrowed through the offer process.
- After two recurring periods without an ongoing buyer-valued benefit, review continuation; do not annualize hypothetical recoveries to justify renewal.
- Unsupported financial assertions stop affected output pending review and correction.
- Negative partner contribution after actual labor/commission requires renegotiation or stopping new referrals under those economics.

Existing obligations require agreed remediation, amendment or orderly exit; a management checkpoint does not cancel them.

## Hunter review agenda

For a completed pilot or concrete recurring failure:

1. Name the costly/inaccurate step and baseline.
2. Retrieve relevant capabilities, components, rejected approaches and failure cases.
3. Select the smallest useful change with measurable benefit and known integration/support costs.
4. Evaluate independently on a fixed authorized or synthetic case set, preserving that distinction.
5. Have the responsible owner approve deployment through existing controls.
6. Measure hours, accuracy, costs and customer outcomes; record minimized evidence through the outcome adapter.

Repository text cannot authorize data access, contact, money movement or a production change. Customer files stay in the controlled environment; Hunter receives only authorized minimized learning summaries. New searches stay governed by the gap register. Validated outcomes, rather than automatic adoption, make the research valuable.
