# Freight Recovery — Commercial Playbook

Updated: 2026-09-23

Status: internal operating guidance. It does not create customer authorization
or replace a signed engagement.

## Core message

> Find out whether freight money may be recoverable without paying an upfront
> audit fee. We analyze an agreed set of freight records and provide a concise
> opportunity summary. If you authorize us to pursue supported opportunities,
> our fee is an agreed percentage of funds actually recovered. If nothing is
> recovered, there is no recovery fee.

Use calm, supportable language. Never promise savings or a refund, call an
estimate recovered cash, or cite an unevidenced result.

## Standard buyer flow

1. **Free-audit request.** Capture business metadata through the public form.
2. **Fit review.** Apply the qualification route and capacity screen.
3. **Secure intake.** Send an approved route and a bounded record request. Do
   not accept customer files through the public site or ordinary email.
4. **Opportunity assessment.** Analyze the agreed records with reviewer and
   provenance controls.
5. **Free summary.** Report records reviewed, opportunity categories, a
   supportable potential range, confidence, limitations, and recommendation.
6. **Recovery offer.** If worthwhile, present scope, attribution, authorizations,
   configurable contingency percentage, fee-eligible recovery definition,
   timing, data terms, termination, and circumvention treatment.
7. **Execution.** Build the detailed claim package and conduct only approved
   external actions after acceptance.
8. **Settlement.** Reconcile customer-received credits, refunds, or remittances,
   then calculate the fee from eligible actual recovered funds.

There is no public checkout in this path. An optional fixed-fee forensic audit
may be proposed by written custom scope when the customer wants a complete
analysis deliverable and to retain all recoveries.

## Qualification conversation

Ask only what changes the routing decision:

- What prompted the review?
- Who owns freight costs, records, and the decision?
- What are the approximate freight-spend and shipment-volume bands?
- Which modes and major carriers are involved?
- How much invoice history and approximately how many invoices are available?
- Which invoices, agreements/rate sheets, shipment records, carrier statements,
  payment records, and prior-audit outputs can be produced?
- Has this period already been audited or are claims active?
- Are overcharges or disputes already suspected?
- What security or provider restrictions apply to the data?

Record unknowns as unknown. A large spend estimate alone does not qualify a
lead, and interest is not an audit result.

## Free summary template

> **Audit population:** [records, date range, modes, carriers, exclusions]
>
> **What appears to exist:** [categories and counts without disclosing the
> proprietary rule set]
>
> **Potential recovery range:** [supportable range] — an estimate, not an
> approved claim or recovered cash.
>
> **Confidence and limitations:** [high/medium/low with the evidence that drives
> it and material missing sources]
>
> **Recommended path:** [no action / obtain missing records / recovery
> engagement]
>
> **Next step if worthwhile:** authorize Freight Recovery to validate and pursue
> the identified opportunities under written success-based terms. The exact
> rate and eligible recovery definition are confirmed before work begins.

Do not attach the complete rules, claim-ready workbook, carrier narrative,
evidence bundle, or correspondence plan to the free summary.

## Recovery proposal fields

Leave an unknown visible rather than inventing it:

- buyer/entity and decision objective;
- records, period, modes, carriers, currencies, and exclusions;
- potential-recovery range, clearly labeled;
- approved activity and external-action owners;
- engagement-specific contingency rate;
- definition and evidence of fee-eligible recovered funds;
- pre-existing matters, automatic credits, incumbent-known claims, duplicates,
  unsupported amounts, reversals, and attribution window;
- invoicing and payment timing;
- confidentiality, data use/retention, termination, and handling of in-flight or
  later recoveries; and
- reasonable non-circumvention treatment for recoveries arising from Freight
  Recovery findings.

Formal terms require legal review. The website framework is explanatory only.

## Objection handling

**“How can the audit be free?”**  The initial analysis is bounded. We invest in
qualified opportunities and make most of our money only when an authorized
recovery succeeds.

**“Can we recover it ourselves?”**  The customer controls its decision. The free
summary is designed for evaluation, not as a substitute for the detailed claim
preparation, evidence package, correspondence, and recovery execution covered
by an engagement.

**“Why share a recovery?”**  Success-based pricing avoids a large upfront audit
decision and aligns the principal fee with realized results. Buyers who prefer
directly funded forensic work may request custom fixed-fee scope.

**“What if you find nothing?”**  The customer owes no audit fee and no recovery
fee. A clean result is not a failure of evidence controls and must not be turned
into an unsupported savings claim.

## Capacity and profit controls

- Limit each free audit by records, checks, history, and analyst/reviewer hours.
- Stage large populations rather than analyzing everything before fit is known.
- Prioritize evidence-ready opportunities with a credible economic buyer.
- Measure all labor, support, secure-intake, and recovery-administration cost.
- Review attribution and exclusions before spending on execution.
- Decline or narrow work whose probability-adjusted eligible fee cannot support
  responsible delivery.
- Never lower review integrity to improve apparent conversion or recovery.

## Proof and claims

The site has a reserved case-study area but no invented proof. Add a case only
after written permission and reconciliation of the audit population, potential
range, approved claim value, actual recovered funds, fee treatment, timeframe,
and material limitations. Anonymization does not eliminate the need for an
evidence trail.

## Funnel events

The public client may emit landing view, CTA, form start, step completion, form
completion, request prepared, email opened, and controlled-demo download.
Systems with authoritative knowledge may later record secure route issued, data
submitted, qualified lead, audit completed, opportunity identified, engagement
started/accepted, and actual recovery recorded. Do not fire downstream success
events merely because a visitor clicked a marketing button.
