# Founding RETALLY Program

Version: 1.0
Launch: 2026-10-06
Capacity: first 3 qualified customers

## Objective

The Founding RETALLY Program is designed to convert the current evidence-first product into real customer outcomes without fabricating proof or maximizing early margin.

Primary outcome chain:

real audit -> supported finding -> customer authorization -> recovery action -> settlement -> verified actual recovery -> optional permissioned case study

## Offer

For the first three qualified customers:
- $0 bounded historical freight audit;
- priority review queue;
- no subscription;
- no implementation fee;
- no per-shipment fee;
- Recovery Evidence Pack included;
- Second-Look Recovery Audit available when an incumbent audit/TMS/payment process already exists;
- customer approval required before outside recovery action;
- contingency rate discounted from the then-current standard commercial rate and confirmed in writing before recovery work;
- no recovery fee when eligible actual recovered funds are $0.

## Qualification

A founding customer should normally have:
- U.S. business operations;
- meaningful parcel and/or LTL freight activity;
- at least 6 months of usable historical records;
- identifiable invoice/shipment records;
- available or recoverable rate authority;
- payment/credit evidence sufficient to reconcile outcomes;
- a finance/AP/logistics owner willing to define scope;
- no exclusive restriction preventing the agreed audit/recovery work.

Preferred company profile:
- approximately $50M-$3B revenue;
- distributor or multi-site shipper;
- multiple branches/DCs or meaningful shipment complexity;
- internal AP/finance;
- HVACR, plumbing/PVF, electrical, building materials, industrial/MRO, fasteners, packaging, safety/JanSan, plastics, welding/gases, or food distribution.

## Case-study option

Case-study permission is never required to receive the founding offer.

After a successful externally evidenced recovery, RETALLY may separately offer an additional commercial concession in exchange for written permission to publish an agreed case study.

The customer controls:
- whether its name is used;
- which figures may be published;
- whether the case is anonymized;
- final approval of attributed statements.

No testimonial or logo use is implied by the recovery engagement.

## Founding customer deliverables

1. bounded audit scope;
2. opportunity summary;
3. Recovery Evidence Pack for validated findings;
4. customer authorization register;
5. recovery-status ledger;
6. settlement/reversal reconciliation;
7. final actual-recovery summary;
8. optional case-study draft only after permission.

## Success criteria

Program success is not "three logos."

Success means at least one founding audit reaches externally evidenced recovery or produces a defensible no-recovery conclusion that validates the methodology.

Track:
- qualified founding conversations;
- audits authorized;
- populations reviewed;
- findings surviving human review;
- claims authorized;
- settlements;
- reversals;
- net actual recovered funds;
- fee-eligible recovery;
- time to first supported finding;
- time to settlement;
- customer referral/case-study permission.

## Capacity rule

Do not publicly represent more than three founding-program slots as filled unless real customers have actually qualified and accepted.

Do not create fake scarcity countdowns or fictional enrollment counts.
