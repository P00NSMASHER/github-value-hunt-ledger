## Referral conversation opener

“We independently test freight billing against the commercial and shipment evidence, then separate potential recovery from actual recovered funds. The first audit is $0 upfront, so it can be a low-friction second look for a client with material freight spend.”

## What not to promise

- no guaranteed recovery percentage;
- no guaranteed savings;
- no referral payment until separately reviewed and agreed;
- no carrier relationship claims;
- no automatic right to contact a client’s carriers;
- no request for confidential records before an approved intake route.

## Target partner qualification

Strong:
- serves distributors, manufacturers, multi-site retailers or shippers;
- works with AP, finance, procurement or logistics data;
- has trusted executive relationships;
- can recognize freight-heavy clients.

Weak:
- purely consumer accounting;
- no access to freight-heavy businesses;
- direct competitor to freight audit/recovery;
- requires upfront partnership fees.

First milestone: 10 referral-partner conversations.
