# Freight Recovery Evidence Standard

Version 1.0 — 2026-10-06

1. Freeze the audit population before measuring results.
2. Establish invoice and shipment identity.
3. Resolve the controlling commercial authority.
4. Apply the authority effective at the relevant shipment date.
5. Recalculate expected charges independently and reproducibly.
6. Route material ambiguity to human review.
7. Exclude pre-existing, automatic, incumbent-known, and duplicate value from Freight Recovery attribution.
8. Require customer authorization before outside recovery action.
9. Link each settlement to the validated economic finding and account for reversals.
10. Call value recovered only when an eligible realized benefit actually posts.

Candidate differences, validated findings, approved claims, settlements, reversals, net actual recovery, and fee-eligible recovery remain separate states.

For Second-Look Recovery Audits, existing controls receive credit for their own known findings and credits before Freight Recovery measures net-new value.

Automation may assist ingestion, matching, calculation, prioritization, and evidence assembly. Material ambiguity remains subject to human review.

This is an operating methodology, not a legal opinion, accounting standard, certification, carrier rule, or guarantee of recovery.
