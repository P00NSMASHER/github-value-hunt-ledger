# Freight Recovery ICP v1.0

Effective: 2026-10-06

## Primary ICP

U.S. distributors and multi-site shippers, approximately $50M-$3B revenue, with meaningful LTL and/or parcel spend, multiple locations or distribution centers, multiple carriers, internal AP/finance, and at least six months of auditable history.

## Priority verticals

1. HVACR
2. plumbing / PVF
3. electrical distribution
4. building materials
5. industrial / MRO
6. fasteners
7. packaging
8. safety / JanSan
9. plastics
10. welding / gases
11. food distribution

## Why this segment

These businesses combine:
- repetitive carrier invoice volume;
- distributed shipping/receiving;
- accessorial exposure;
- contract/rate complexity;
- LTL and parcel adjustment risk;
- internal finance teams that can validate credits and settlements;
- enough scale for meaningful recovery without automatically creating Fortune-50 procurement friction.

## Fit score — 100 points

### Freight complexity — 30
- 10: multiple modes, especially LTL + parcel
- 10: multiple branches/DCs
- 10: multiple carriers / decentralized shipping

### Auditability — 25
- 10: invoice/shipment exports available
- 10: contracts/rate schedules/rate confirmations available
- 5: payment/credit evidence available

### Economic potential — 20
- 10: estimated company scale within target band
- 10: meaningful shipment/freight-volume proxies

### Buyer accessibility — 15
- 5: identifiable finance/AP owner
- 5: identifiable logistics/transportation owner
- 5: reachable official business channel

### Trigger / urgency — 10
- acquisitions, branch/DC expansion, carrier changes, rapid growth, system changes, audit-provider transition, or visible freight complexity

## Offer routing

### First-Look Recovery Audit
Use when no mature freight-audit/payment process is evident.

### Second-Look Recovery Audit
Prefer when the company already uses:
- a TMS;
- freight-payment/audit provider;
- internal freight-audit workflow;
- 3PL audit service;
- carrier reconciliation process.

Existing controls are not an objection. They define the delta boundary.

## Disqualifiers / penalties

Penalize:
- direct freight-audit competitors;
- companies whose primary business overlaps our audit/recovery service;
- tiny freight populations;
- inaccessible commercial authority;
- no usable historical records;
- exclusive incumbent restrictions;
- extreme enterprise procurement friction unless expected recovery justifies it.

## Buyer priority

1. CFO / VP Finance
2. Controller
3. AP leader
4. Transportation / logistics leader
5. Procurement / strategic sourcing
6. Operations leader where freight ownership is explicit

## Required top-25 account brief

For each account record:
- company;
- sector;
- estimated scale band;
- branch/DC footprint;
- likely freight modes;
- likely freight complexity;
- public evidence of TMS/audit/payment provider, if any;
- recent expansion/acquisition/DC/system trigger;
- CFO/controller;
- AP leader;
- transportation/logistics leader;
- procurement leader;
- official contact route;
- first-look vs second-look recommendation;
- personalization sentence;
- evidence links;
- confidence;
- outreach state.

Prospect-level data must not be committed to the public repository.
