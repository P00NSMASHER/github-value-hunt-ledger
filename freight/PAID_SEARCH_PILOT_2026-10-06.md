# Freight Recovery Paid Search Pilot — 2026-10-06

Status: PREPARED / NOT YET LAUNCHED

## Decision

Run a small Google Search pilot in parallel with outbound email. Do not start with LinkedIn, display, or broad social ads.

Reason:
- Search captures existing buyer intent for freight audit/recovery.
- Outbound tests buyer accessibility; paid search tests inbound demand.
- The current business has no verified customer case study yet, so broad awareness advertising is premature.
- LinkedIn's current minimum daily campaign budget is materially higher and requires a longer learning window.

## Budget

Initial test: **$10-$15 average daily budget for 14 days**.

Target spend:
- low case: $140
- high case: $210

Do not raise budget before at least one of these occurs:
- qualified inbound lead;
- secure-intake-ready buyer;
- evidence that high-intent keywords are generating relevant clicks at an acceptable cost.

## Success threshold

The pilot is promising if 14 days produces:
- at least 1 qualified conversation, OR
- at least 2 credible inbound leads that match the ICP.

The pilot is weak if:
- spend reaches $200 with no credible inquiry;
- search terms are dominated by irrelevant informational traffic;
- landing-page clicks do not produce any buyer-intent action.

## Stop rules

Pause immediately if:
- irrelevant search terms dominate;
- ad policy/identity issues appear;
- conversion tracking cannot distinguish real leads from CTA clicks;
- cost rises materially without qualified engagement.

## LinkedIn

Do not launch LinkedIn paid ads yet.

Revisit after:
- one verified customer outcome or case study, OR
- at least 3 qualified sales conversations from outbound/search.

Use LinkedIn organically for targeted decision-maker outreach before paid LinkedIn advertising.
