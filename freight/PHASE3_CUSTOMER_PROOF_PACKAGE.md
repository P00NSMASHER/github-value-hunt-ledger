# RecoveryOS — Customer Proof Package

Prepared: 2026-10-07  
Status: **real-customer proof protocol; no real-customer outcome is asserted**

## Objective

Convert RecoveryOS's strongest internal advantages, second-look attribution and
financial evidence genealogy, into buyer-controlled external proof.

## Required proof chain

A real proof package must contain:

1. executed engagement/scope authorization;
2. buyer + business-unit identity;
3. data-room/source manifest;
4. frozen population hash;
5. sealed incumbent-source hash;
6. buyer-owned truth manifest frozen before incumbent opening;
7. RecoveryOS finding output;
8. buyer review dispositions;
9. exact authority/source locators;
10. false-positive findings and dollars;
11. unresolved findings and dollars;
12. reviewer time/touches;
13. approved carrier/vendor actions;
14. carrier/provider responses;
15. buyer-controlled settlement/credit/remittance evidence;
16. reversals;
17. net actual recovery;
18. fee-eligible realized recovery;
19. final package hash;
20. buyer acceptance/challenge record.

## Blind accuracy rule

The independent truth owner must not see RecoveryOS classifications before the
truth set is frozen.

Without that ordering, do not report false-positive or false-negative rates as
blind validation.

## Minimum real-customer metrics

- total frozen invoices/shipments;
- mode/carrier coverage;
- authority coverage;
- unsupported/review rate;
- false-positive count/dollars;
- independently discovered false negatives;
- precision/recall where truth is defensible;
- exact-dollar error;
- challenger-only validated dollars;
- incumbent-known excluded dollars;
- duplicate/pre-existing/automatic excluded dollars;
- reviewer minutes / 1,000 invoices;
- realized recovery;
- reversed recovery;
- fee-eligible realized recovery;
- time to first finding;
- time to first realized outcome.

## Success states

### AUDIT_QUALITY_PROVEN
Buyer accepts the blinded truth methodology and false-dollar behavior, even if
the population is clean.

### INCREMENTAL_FINDING_PROVEN
At least one independently validated challenger-only finding exists.

### REALIZED_RECOVERY_PROVEN
At least one challenger-only finding reaches buyer-controlled settlement
evidence with unique allocation.

### REPEATABILITY_PROVEN
A second distinct population/buyer period reproduces acceptable quality and
operational effort.

## What does not qualify as customer proof

- synthetic demo;
- AI buyer persona;
- internal gold fixture;
- vendor promise;
- submitted dispute;
- carrier promise to credit;
- estimated annualized recovery;
- marketing testimonial not tied to evidence;
- data from another population;
- a "customer" invented by us because reality was taking too long.

## Reference customer gate

A buyer becomes referenceable only with explicit permission defining:

- name/logo permission;
- anonymous vs named case study;
- exact metrics allowed;
- review/expiration rights;
- whether security/architecture details may be disclosed.

Until then, the existence and outcome remain private.
