# RecoveryOS — ROI & Commercial Evidence Methodology

Prepared: 2026-10-07

## Principle

RecoveryOS ROI is calculated from **uniquely attributable realized value**, not
from every discrepancy the software happens to notice.

## Money states

Keep these separate:

1. reviewed discrepancy;
2. validated finding;
3. challenger-only validated;
4. approved claim/action;
5. settled/credited;
6. reversed;
7. net actual recovery;
8. fee-eligible realized recovery.

## Canonical formulas

### Candidate leakage
`sum(max(billed - expected, 0))`

Not ROI. Not savings.

### Challenger-only validated
Validated positive findings absent from the frozen incumbent-known universe.

Still not realized cash.

### Net actual recovery
`gross uniquely allocated settlement - reversals`

### Fee-eligible realized recovery
Net actual recovery after excluding:

- incumbent-known;
- pre-existing claims;
- automatic credits;
- duplicate opportunities;
- contractually excluded categories.

### Buyer net benefit
`net actual recovery - Freight fees - buyer incremental operating cost`

### Pilot ROI
`buyer net benefit / buyer incremental operating cost`

Report the denominator explicitly. If buyer operating cost is not measured,
report dollars recovered and Freight fee separately instead of inventing a ratio.

## Labor economics

Record:

- invoices/shipments;
- reviewer touches;
- reviewer minutes;
- engineering/integration hours;
- customer IT hours where measurable;
- turnaround;
- external recovery follow-up hours.

Useful metrics:

- reviewer minutes / 1,000 invoices;
- validated dollars / reviewer hour;
- realized dollars / reviewer hour;
- customer IT hours to launch;
- time to first defensible finding;
- time to first realized recovery.

## Clean population

A clean population may show good audit quality but **zero recovery ROI** for the
tested period. Do not convert "we found nothing unsupported" into avoided spend.

## Synthetic simulation boundary

AI-buyer simulations and synthetic gold findings contribute **$0** to:

- revenue;
- recovered dollars;
- willingness to pay;
- customer ROI;
- referenceability.

They exist only to test whether the buying/diligence package survives skeptical
questions.

## Commercial decision rule

A broader engagement is economically supported only after a real buyer accepts
the proof semantics and either:

- observes uniquely attributable realized recovery; or
- values the independent assurance/clean-population result enough to purchase
  continued coverage.

The spreadsheet must wait for reality. Tragic, but financially useful.
