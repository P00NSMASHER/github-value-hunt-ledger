## Reproducibility

- Frozen generator seed: `20261022`. Source fixture reference is a **synthetic generator**, not a confidential data download.
- `freight/fixtures/priority4_synthetic_stress_baseline_v1.json`: fixed protocol, 3,072-case receipt-list SHA-256 and independently checked counts/coverage. Any changed case, algorithm or expected outcome requires a reviewed fixture/version change; never silently update it to make tests green.
- Each SQLite case owns its own temporary database. No real carrier contracts, claims, credentials, payment rails, legal findings, cash receipts or actual customers are fetched, stored or simulated as real.
- **All 3,072 source-free samples remain unresolved/conditional decisions**. Source-declared credits in 120 SQLite rehearsals are not verified settlement, bank money, confirmed recoveries or independently adjudicated freight truth.
- Immutable customer or buyer identity, source SHA-256, negative and positive test expectations, full denominators, source-head SHAs and exact CI outcomes must be preserved and supplied to QA & Delivery.
- Memory, schedules, protected release rules, Work/Codex credit use, production, real communications and customer/private financial systems are outside scope.

## Necessary limits and release classification

Passing deterministic synthetic tests **cannot establish a zero-defect engine**, accurate original freight overcharge detection, real legal collectibility, empirical recovery probability or permission to start a pilot. Real original court PDF invoice faces (100 GlobalTranz + 10 ArcBest) still lack authenticated full independent positive/negative truth labels; 110 freight-error labels remain `UNKNOWN`, not 110 true negatives. Pennine's same-source accounting reconciliation is **not** freight detection accuracy.

All true payment/customer financial action paths retain authorization, independently authenticated cash, signed rights, legal notice, reviewer adjudication and privacy release gates. The existing standalone buyer launch authorization stays **NO-GO**. Neither CI results nor a self-reported test role can override it.

**Acceptance condition for technical Priority 4 completion:** new deterministic/property/mutation/transaction tests passing on Windows and Linux, exact source and fixture hashes matching protected main, complete pre-merge and post-merge required CI, no new real payments/services, and truthful no-go qualification preserved. A main merge alone is not proof of actual customer/commercial success.
