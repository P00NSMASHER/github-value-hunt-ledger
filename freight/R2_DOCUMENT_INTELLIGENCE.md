# RETALLY R2 — original-document intelligence (staged, not production enabled)

The original-PDF extractor is an isolated, source-pinned template for two
historical bankruptcy exhibits only. It parses original invoice faces, records
per-page field witnesses, and replays GlobalTranz positive charges and negative
discounts through R1's typed exact-cent financial model.

Acceptance: 100/100 original GlobalTranz invoice totals, including 93 invoices
with typed discounts; 10/10 original ArcBest face amounts. ArcBest still lacks
verified line-level itemization and original invoice issue dates. Neither
source supplies governing buyer tariff, independent actual shipment weights,
bank-cleared payment or independent freight overcharge adjudication.

Actual source bytes are fetched temporarily by read-only GitHub CI and never
committed, logged, uploaded or distributed. Unknown or modified PDFs fail closed.
A general-purpose customer-facing OCR/PDF service, malware sandbox, document
rights gate, field confidence calibration and independent human extraction
review remain separate launch acceptance blockers. No commercial use or
freight accuracy/cash claim is enabled by this PR.
