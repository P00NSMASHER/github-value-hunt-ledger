# RETALLY Referral Partner Program

Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-10-06

## Objective

Build a repeatable referral channel through advisors who already serve freight-heavy distributors, manufacturers, logistics operators, and multi-site shippers.

Priority partner types:
1. fractional CFO firms;
2. regional CPA/accounting/advisory firms;
3. AP and finance-process consultants;
4. procurement advisors;
5. TMS implementation/selection consultants;
6. ERP consultants serving distributors/manufacturers;
7. transportation/logistics consultants;
8. warehouse/distribution consultants;
9. PE operating partners;
10. 3PLs that do not sell competing freight-audit/recovery services.

## Partner proposition

RETALLY provides an evidence-first historical freight audit for the partner's client.

- $0 upfront audit;
- no subscription required;
- customer approves recovery actions;
- Recovery Evidence Pack documents validated findings;
- Second-Look option when the client already uses a TMS, freight-payment provider, or incumbent auditor;
- fees are tied to eligible actual recovered funds under the engagement.

The partner does not need to perform the freight audit or recovery work.

## Referral positioning

Use:

> We independently review historical freight billing for supportable overcharges and missed credits. Your client can start with a $0-upfront audit, and you do not need to perform the recovery work. If they already audit freight, our Second-Look review measures only supportable net-new value after existing findings and credits are accounted for.

Do not:
- promise a recovery;
- imply a referral fee exists before written terms;
- imply the partner endorses RETALLY before it does;
- use the partner's logo without permission;
- ask the partner to send customer freight files by ordinary email.

## Partner qualification

Prefer partners whose client base includes:
- manufacturing;
- wholesale/distribution;
- industrial/MRO;
- building materials;
- HVACR/plumbing/electrical;
- food distribution;
- logistics/warehousing;
- PE-backed multi-site operators.

Good signals:
- CFO/controller services;
- working-capital or margin advisory;
- ERP/TMS implementation;
- supply-chain optimization;
- AP transformation;
- distribution/logistics specialization.

Avoid:
- direct freight-audit competitors;
- freight-payment firms whose contract prevents referral;
- partners whose client base is too small for meaningful freight spend;
- firms requiring unsupported exclusivity.

## Partner workflow

RESEARCHED -> VERIFIED_ROUTE -> TOUCH_1 -> RESPONDED -> QUALIFIED_PARTNER -> INTRO_RECEIVED -> CLIENT_QUALIFIED -> AUDIT_AUTHORIZED -> OUTCOME_RECORDED

Any opt-out or no-fit becomes SUPPRESSED.

## Referral economics

Do not publish or promise a referral commission until a written referral agreement has been reviewed and approved.

The initial channel may operate without commissions:
- partner introduces qualified client;
- RETALLY handles audit/recovery;
- partner remains the trusted advisor;
- no fee is owed to the partner unless separately agreed.

If a paid referral model is later adopted, define:
- eligibility;
- attribution;
- payment trigger;
- clawbacks/reversals;
- disclosure requirements;
- client consent where appropriate;
- termination.

## KPIs

Track:
- partners researched;
- verified routes;
- partner replies;
- qualified partners;
- introductions;
- qualified referred clients;
- audits authorized;
- actual recovered funds from referred clients;
- partner-sourced revenue;
- repeat introductions.

The headline metric is qualified introductions, not messages sent.
