# Second-Look Recovery Audit — Operating Brief

Updated: 2026-10-06
Status: ACTIVE PRODUCT WEDGE

## Purpose

Sell an independent post-payment / post-audit review to companies that already have a TMS, freight-payment provider, internal AP controls, carrier reconciliation, 3PL audit service, or incumbent freight-audit vendor.

The offer is not "replace your current system." The offer is:

> Already audit your freight? Good. We independently test what the first audit may have missed.

## Commercial promise

- $0-upfront bounded second-look audit.
- Existing audit infrastructure is accepted as part of the evidence environment.
- Pre-existing, incumbent-known, automatic, duplicate, unsupported, and reversed amounts are excluded from fee-eligible recovery.
- Recovery work requires separate customer authorization.
- Fees apply only to eligible actual recovered funds under the signed engagement.
- No guarantee of findings or recovery.

## Required qualification

Capture:
- buyer / decision owner;
- records owner;
- audit population entity and business unit;
- date range;
- freight modes and major carriers;
- invoice count or volume band;
- approximate freight-spend band;
- current TMS / freight-payment / audit process;
- incumbent audit provider if any;
- whether prior-audit output can be exported or documented;
- known disputes / automatic credits / open claims;
- commercial authority available (contracts, tariffs, rate sheets, rate confirmations, amendments);
- shipment evidence available;
- payment / credit evidence available;
- approved secure intake route.

## Freeze requirement

Before Freight Recovery measures net-new value, freeze:
1. population;
2. exclusions;
3. incumbent-known findings;
4. existing credits;
5. active disputes / claims;
6. relevant incumbent output;
7. controlling commercial sources where available.

The customer should not be asked to disable or alter existing controls for the audit.

## Delta-only attribution

A Freight Recovery finding is not fee-eligible merely because:
- the incumbent system did not surface it in the same format;
- the customer already knew about it;
- an automatic carrier credit was already in flight;
- another auditor had already opened the claim;
- the same economic issue appears on several related records;
- a provisional claim amount has not settled;
- a credit was later reversed.

A valid success-fee candidate requires unique attribution to a Freight Recovery-originated, supported, customer-authorized recovery under the engagement.

## Second-look workflow

1. Qualify the buyer and current control environment.
2. Define and freeze a bounded population.
3. Capture incumbent output / known matters.
4. Build shipment and invoice identity.
5. Resolve controlling authority.
6. Recalculate expected charges independently.
7. Compare actual billing.
8. Route ambiguity to human review.
9. Suppress duplicate / pre-existing / known / automatic outcomes.
10. Deliver a net-new opportunity summary.
11. Obtain explicit customer authorization for recovery.
12. Prepare claim / correspondence package.
13. Track carrier response.
14. Match credit / refund / remittance to the validated finding.
15. Account for reversals.
16. Calculate eligible actual recovered funds.
17. Record the externally evidenced outcome.

## Buyer-facing output

The free second-look summary should report:
- frozen population;
- existing-control context;
- records reviewed;
- opportunity categories;
- supportable net-new potential range when justified;
- suppressed pre-existing / incumbent-known categories;
- confidence / missing evidence;
- recommended next step.

Do not disclose the complete proprietary recovery playbook in the free summary.

## Sales qualification opener

> You already audit your freight? Good. We are not asking you to replace that process. Freight Recovery runs an independent second look against a frozen historical population and measures only supportable, net-new recovery after your existing findings and credits are accounted for.

## Objections

### "We already have a freight-audit provider."
That is the intended use case. The second look tests whether a supportable residual remains after the incumbent process. Existing and known findings are excluded from our recovery attribution.

### "Will this create duplicate claims?"
The workflow explicitly freezes known matters and suppresses duplicate or already-open opportunities before any recovery action.

### "Do we need to replace our TMS or payment provider?"
No. The second look is complementary and bounded.

### "What if you find nothing?"
There is no upfront audit fee and no recovery fee.

- invoices reviewed;
- candidates flagged;
- candidates surviving human review;
- candidates suppressed as pre-existing / known / automatic / duplicate;
- net-new potential range;
- authorized claims;
- actual recovered funds;
- reversals;
- fee-eligible recovered funds;
- time to first supported finding;
- time to settlement;
- analyst/reviewer hours;
- contribution margin.

## Stop / rework signals

- High suppression rate + low net-new yield: narrow the ICP or target weaker incumbent controls.
- Good findings but no authorization: trust / contract / conflict issue.
- Authorized claims but no settlement: recovery-execution issue.
- Repeated inability to capture incumbent output: redesign intake / scope language.
