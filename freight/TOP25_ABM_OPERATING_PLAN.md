# Top-25 Account-Based Sales Operating Plan

Effective: 2026-10-06

## Purpose

Convert the highest-fit Freight Recovery prospects from a generic email list into researched accounts with an explicit buyer, trigger, offer route, and evidence-backed personalization.

Prospect-level names, contact details, and research notes are private operating data and must not be committed to this public repository.

## Account brief schema

Each private account brief must contain:
- rank;
- company;
- sector;
- estimated scale band;
- branch/DC footprint;
- likely freight modes;
- freight-complexity evidence;
- current TMS / freight-payment / audit provider when publicly evidenced;
- recent acquisition / expansion / DC / system trigger;
- CFO / finance executive;
- Controller / AP leader;
- transportation / logistics / supply-chain leader;
- procurement leader;
- official contact route;
- first-look vs Second-Look recommendation;
- one evidence-backed personalization sentence;
- evidence sources;
- confidence;
- outreach state;
- opt-out / suppression state.

Unknown means UNKNOWN. Do not infer an email pattern.

## Offer routing

FIRST-LOOK:
Use when no mature freight-audit/payment control is publicly evident.

SECOND-LOOK:
Use when an existing TMS, audit/payment provider, internal audit program, or other mature control is evident.

Second-Look opener:
> You already audit your freight? Good. We are not asking you to replace that process. We independently test a frozen historical population for supportable, net-new recovery after existing findings and credits are accounted for.

## Buyer order

1. CFO / VP Finance
2. Controller
3. AP leader
4. Transportation / logistics / supply-chain leader
5. Procurement / strategic sourcing

When a named buyer cannot be responsibly verified, use the company's official business contact route and ask for routing rather than inventing an address.

## Account score

Use the 100-point ICP score in ICP_V1.md.

Top-25 execution order:
- Tier A: 80-100 — individualized research and direct buyer outreach.
- Tier B: 65-79 — individualized company trigger + role routing.
- Tier C: below 65 — reserve until higher tiers are exhausted or new evidence improves fit.

## Required pre-send check

Before first touch:
- verify company remains operating;
- verify contact route;
- verify personalization fact;
- confirm no prior opt-out;
- check prior Freight Recovery thread;
- choose first-look or second-look;
- include business identity and postal address;
- include simple opt-out;
- do not request confidential freight records by email.

## Outcome states

RESEARCHED -> READY -> SENT_1 -> REPLIED -> QUALIFIED -> SECURE_INTAKE_READY -> AUDIT_AUTHORIZED -> CLOSED_WON / CLOSED_NO_FIT / SUPPRESSED

Follow-up states:
SENT_1 -> D5_DUE -> SENT_2 -> D12_DUE -> SENT_3 -> CLOSED_NO_REPLY

Any opt-out immediately becomes SUPPRESSED.
