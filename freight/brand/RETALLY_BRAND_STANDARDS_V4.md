# RETALLY — Corporate brand standards v4 (October 8, 2026)
Status: visual system finished; derived asset production and legal/contact gates remain distinct.

## Non-negotiable identity
- Primary: **approved glossy charcoal RETALLY wordmark, with green A**.
- Secondary: **approved glossy emerald-and-charcoal folded R**. Use as a standalone avatar, favicon or selected report-cover element. Never append it to the full wordmark as an additional logo.
- Protect original artwork exactly; do not redraw, recolor, warp, fake highlights or replace letter shapes.
- Tagline: **Find it. Prove it. Recover it.**
- Positioning: **We find freight billing errors, document the evidence, and help recover overpayments.**

The older cobalt-colored site tokens are historically named; avoid renaming integration keys merely to achieve branding. Final CSS layer supplies restrained colors. Prefer white and ink over large solid green sections.

## Typography
Preserve the site's actual **Hanken Grotesk** UI and **Instrument Serif** editorial emphasis already approved on the live homepage. Do not change header hierarchy, button geometry or hero sizing to make a logo swap. Documents use a neutral readable office-compatible font, clear hierarchy and tabular financial figures. Logos are images, never retyped approximations.

## Logo applications
- On white/light backgrounds: full-resolution approved source art or individually QA-approved transparent artwork.
- On dark backgrounds: use a deliberate white logo plate to maintain charcoal-letter visibility; do not put dark metallic letters directly on deep forest or midnight ink.
- Provide at least 0.3× artwork height in clear space. Keep native aspect ratios.
- Test 16/32/48/64px icon renditions and wordmark at 166px width and printed reproduction.
- One-color traced SVG and algorithmic transparent cuts are **approval candidates**, not authoritative vector masters. A human must check silhouettes, counters, glints and halos at 1×/8× before use.
- Originals remain immutable with full SHA-256 hashes in the companion asset register.

## Web, report and email patterns
Keep one primary CTA per section, accessible keyboard focus, and consistent borders and spacing. No stacked sticky audit CTA. No fictional buyer results presented as real.
Customer documents must distinguish **candidate**, **supported**, **customer-authorized**, and **funds actually received**. Fees refer to eligible actual recoveries only as defined in separately signed commercial terms. No carrier outreach or confidential data intake is implied by contact-form preparation.

## Acceptance and publication
Color/spacing update can publish independently of unapproved logo cutouts or legal contract details if exact-head CI and responsive comparison pass. Customer signature remains in review until the inbound mailbox is confirmed and sender details are true. Published URLs, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, the public contact flow, Cloudflare deployment and trading-name/legal clearance require separate recorded evidence.
