## Buyer trust and factual constraints

Potential overcharge, validated finding, approved recovery claim and money actually posted are different. Use customer authorization before outside recovery actions. No guarantees, fabricated recovery figures, unverified credentials, or customer case studies. Keep existing approved contact routes and consent mechanisms in operation.

Before putting RETALLY on legal invoices, W-9 forms or binding contracts as a registered trading entity, complete the relevant Pennsylvania name/DBA, U.S. trademark, common-law and domain diligence. Do not silently alter the legal contracting party. Historic records remain intact.
