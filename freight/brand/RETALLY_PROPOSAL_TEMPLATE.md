# Freight billing review & recovery support

**Find it. Prove it. Recover it.**

## What we will review

A bounded set of carrier invoices and their controlling shipment, rate, contract and credit records. Scope, carriers, periods and exclusions are agreed before a review begins.

## How it works

**Find it.** Compare the eligible carrier invoices against shipment and rate records.  
**Prove it.** Document each supportable discrepancy and its governing commercial authority.  
**Recover it.** Only with separate written authorization, help pursue eligible overpayments and reconcile what actually posts.

## Your outcome

A concise audit summary, supporting evidence for valid findings, open evidence gaps, a recovery-action decision path, and a status separating claims from funds received.

## Commercial terms

The initial recovery audit is $0 upfront for the published eligible scope. Any subsequent recovery fee, exclusions, success definition, timing and authorization will be set out in written engagement terms. **We do not guarantee recovery.** This draft is not a binding contract.
