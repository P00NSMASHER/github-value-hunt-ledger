# RETALLY | Adviser and referral partner brief

**Who it serves:** CPA and accounting firms, fractional CFOs, AP consultants and logistics advisers whose clients have significant freight spend.

**Offer:** Independent review of historical invoice charges for evidence-supported duplicates, rate variances, unsupported accessorials and missed credits.

**Introduction workflow:** Obtain customer permission, confirm scope, agree secure intake, then review and present supported findings. Do not email confidential customer records.

**Commercial boundary:** Eligible initial review is $0 upfront. Any recovery work requires separate authorization, signed terms and eligibility conditions. Fees apply to eligible actual recovery, not flags or uncollected claims. No referral fee is implied; any such arrangement requires specific written disclosure.
