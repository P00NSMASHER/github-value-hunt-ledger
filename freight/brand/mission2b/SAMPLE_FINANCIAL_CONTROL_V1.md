## Separate reproducible worked scenario (NOT INCLUDED IN AGGREGATE)
All identifying data and source IDs are invented solely for teaching the calculation:
- Northstar Industrial Supply; Blue River Freight; shipment FR-1001.
- Contract/rate source `RATE-FR-2026-A`: base transportation **$820**, fuel **18% of $820 = $147.60**, authorized liftgate **$75**.
- Expected bill = 820 + 147.60 + 75 = **$1,042.60**.
- Actual illustrative carrier invoice `INV-FR-1001` = **$1,167.60**.
- Unsupported second liftgate = **$125.00**; difference = 1,167.60 - 1,042.60 = **$125.00**.
- A flag becomes **supported** only on source review. Specific written approval is required before claim; a carrier-accepted claim is not recovered money. A posted $125 credit would create receipt, reversed if canceled.
- Fake demonstration IDs `POD-FR-1001`, `AUTH-FR-1001`, `CR-FR-1001` are NOT links to actual evidence.

## Training-only pricing illustration
If a separately assumed **$125** becomes fee-eligible and the hypothetical rate is **20%**, arithmetic fee = **$25**, customer remainder before operating costs = **$100**. Another arbitrary training scenario of $1,000 at 20% yields a $200 fee and $800 remainder. **Neither is RETALLY pricing, a proposal, or signed commercial terms.**
