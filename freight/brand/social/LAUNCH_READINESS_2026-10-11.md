## Social → website → inquiry: what is actually observable

1. **Meta exposure:** Actual post permalink, published timestamp, organic reach and meaningful actions. Must verify after each scheduled slot; PENDING is not publication.
2. **Social traffic:** Facebook posts carry campaign/source UTM links. Instagram feed captions point to the profile link because plain URLs are not clickable there. Instagram profile URL was previously confirmed as the canonical site, but per-profile UTM attribution needs a live edit and verification if desired.
3. **On-site intent:** The public homepage presents clear pricing, process, sample-not-client-results disclosure and the “Start Free Recovery Audit” CTA at https://www.retallyrecovery.com/#start-audit.
4. **Important actual conversion behavior:** The production qualification form currently **prepares an email draft; it does not automatically send an inquiry**. The visitor must open, review and press Send in their own email app. Do not mark “request received” from a draft or form interaction. The public page discloses this explicitly. Production automated intake remains blocked until separately authorized and tested.
5. **Qualified lead:** Count only an inquiry **actually received** and reviewed by the business mailbox. Distinguish inquiry, scope fit, records transferred by approved route, separately authorized recovery and actual recovered funds.
6. **Attribution limit:** Tagged links alone do not prove visits. Do not report website sessions, audit starts, qualified leads or campaign ROI until an actual authorized analytics source and mailbox evidence support those numbers.
