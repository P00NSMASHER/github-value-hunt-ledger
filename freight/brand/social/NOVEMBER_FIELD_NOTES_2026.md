**Prepared:** October 11, 2026. **Status:** Four `draft=true`, `autoPublish=false` entries; NONE is an active publication. **Metricool brand:** `7322512`. **Production impact:** October 12–27 Facebook/Instagram auto-publication campaign was not changed.

## Editorial direction

Modern freight-finance education that earns saves and thoughtful engagement. Use premium 4:5 branded graphics rather than unbranded stock photos. Each topic consists of **four 1080×1350 PNG slides** with restrained editorial typography, emerald/charcoal and mint palettes, an existing RETALLY brand-lettering bitmap, noninvented terminology, and a final saveable checklist.

Content is written for AP managers, transportation teams, controllers and freight buyers—not general-consumer humor. Rate discrepancies and accessorial flags are **investigation candidates**, never automatically errors, claim approvals or recovered cash. All claims depend on contracts, effective dates, shipment evidence, corrections and credits.

**Provenance:** The slide lettering was sourced from an existing reviewed RETALLY graphic, preserved as image pixels (not generated logo text). Original website-approved PNG logo masters remain canonical for future brand edits. No customer, carrier account, proprietary contract, private invoice, promised recovery or testimonial appears on any slide. Source generator + local PNGs + manifest are bundled in the separately delivered conversation artifact `RETALLY_November_Field_Notes.zip`; source fonts are not bundled.
