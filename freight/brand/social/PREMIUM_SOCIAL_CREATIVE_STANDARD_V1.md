_Approved visually by owner, October 11, 2026. Applies to new RETALLY organic social artwork unless specifically superseded._

## One-sentence brief
Produce genuinely premium, high-gloss, cinematic, visually coherent freight-finance graphics that tell a busy business buyer **what RETALLY does in plain English** immediately. Do not substitute decorative effects for comprehension.

## Design rules
1. **Composition:** 4:5 vertical feed design, production target **1080 × 1350 px**, with text/logo away from crop-prone edges. The actual approved raster master is 1122 × 1402; preserve its aspect on export and inspect platform rehosting, rather than stretch it.
2. **Finish:** deep black/charcoal/emerald, restrained mint highlights, strong clean light/dark separation, glossy material cues, sharp reflections, subtle cinematic gold/industrial backlight. Appearance should be premium, not plastic or cartoonish.
3. **Typography:** modern geometric sans-serif and strong editorial headline weights; generous letter-spacing for the RETALLY wordmark only, compact precise body copy. Avoid dated serif headline styles, generic slide fonts, exaggerated outlines, and tiny explanatory paragraphs.
4. **3D/glass cards:** if content needs a sequence, use 3–4 aligned cards with **small raised bevels**, translucent deep-green glass, subtle rim highlights, physically plausible soft shadows and modern line icons. Keep bevel depth restrained; the words matter most.
5. **Freight visual:** polished tractor-trailer/container-terminal scene, crisp believable truck detail, wet-ground reflections if appropriate, optional subtle network globe. **No RETALLY logo/text on the truck windshield or front glass.** Do not invent logos of real shippers or carrier partners.
6. **Information hierarchy:** brand → one benefit headline → one short factual explanation → optionally 3–4 process steps → one clear CTA/tagline. Preserve useful whitespace. No decorative side slogans, filler buzzwords, fake dashboards, random world maps, or tiny hard-to-read legal caveats.
7. **Brand line:** "Find it. Prove it. Recover it." Use only if legible. When promoting the initial audit, qualify as **"$0 upfront initial audit for agreed eligible scope"**; separately authorized recovery work and no guarantees must be disclosed in copy where relevant.
8. **Phone-first QA:** verify a 390px iPhone viewport. Headlines, numbers and cards must remain readable, no clipped words/text bleeding into photography, no brand mismatch, and good contrast. A direct public image load is not evidence of the social post going live.
9. **Topic specificity:** share one coherent house style but create **different appropriate artwork per topic**—rate mismatch, duplicate/rebill, accessorial, evidence, already-issued credit, fee explanation, or audit process. Do not stamp the same four-step intro visual on every unrelated post. Never let the image promise more than the caption can support.
10. **Clean claims:** avoid fake customer screenshots, invented recoveries, guaranteed refunds, made-up average savings, fabricated reviews and confidential invoices. Label hypothetical examples **ILLUSTRATIVE / SAMPLE — NOT A CUSTOMER RESULT**. Never say a flagged discrepancy is a validated or paid recovery.

## Operational maintenance
- First download/reuse the **approved master**; don't regenerate it each time. For a new topic, use it as a **style reference**, keeping its design language but writing new specific content.
- Verify any new rendered graphic visually before changing a live post or publishing. Text must be letter-perfect, including disclaimers.
- If replacing existing media, fetch the latest Metricool post by immutable `uuid`, change only the media/accurate alt text and any required AI flag, preserve title/caption/date/time/type/links/automatic publishing. Metricool assigns a new numerical `id` after updates; verify the new readback.
- **Original historical baseline remains** `performance/baselines/retally-2026-10-11.json`; approved-revision baseline is `performance/baselines/retally-2026-10-12-creative-approved.json`. Use the newer revision for present-day queue comparisons but retain the original when auditing changes.
- **No automatic sweeping retrofit:** the other 11 October posts and all four November 4-slide drafts remain unchanged. New art needs topic-specific approval/readability QA before individual replacement. November drafts stay `draft=true` / `autoPublish=false` until separately authorized.
- Confirm a real public Facebook/Instagram permalink after the due date to establish publication. Planner PENDING does not prove delivery. No paid spend, customer files, private documents, DMs or scheduled ChatGPT tasks are authorized by this style guide.
