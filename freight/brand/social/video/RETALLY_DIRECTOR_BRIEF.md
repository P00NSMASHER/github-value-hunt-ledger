### Reference technique (27.1-second owner-supplied screen recording)

- **0:00–0:09:** a relaxed, confident presenter in an intentional, professionally lit studio. Realistic mouth movement, natural gestures, eye contact, and a real microphone give the words credibility. No talking mannequin or lip-sync mismatch.
- **~0:09–0:22:** split-screen, with the presenter **anchoring the lower half**, while moving b-roll above the speaker demonstrates the spoken point; cut only when information changes.
- **~0:22–0:27:** the presenter resolves the thought; cut to a restrained branded end card for the final beat.
- Short, accurately synchronized *sentence fragments* sit above mobile navigation controls; no oversize repeated phrases covering faces or evidence. The source Reel's original personality, brand, voice, footage, music and facial likeness **must not be reused** in RETALLY creative.

## Locked concept: “90 minutes billed. 30 minutes supported?”

**Use a clearly labeled illustrative case**, not a real customer or a reported RETALLY recovery. The facts below are hypothetical for educational purposes. In real freight agreements, the controlling tariff, clock start, appointment rules and exceptions can change the calculation.

- Hypothetical gate arrival: **08:30**.
- Hypothetical departure: **11:00**.
- Dwell: **150 minutes** (11:00 − 08:30).
- Hypothetical contractual free time: **120 minutes**, explicitly starting at arrival for this educational example.
- Time beyond that allowance: **30 minutes** (150 − 120).
- Illustrative invoice bills detention for **90 minutes**.
- Candidate variance: **60 minutes** (90 − 30), pending contract-version, appointment, exceptions, logs, prior adjustments, credits and authority checks.
- **Never** describe 60 minutes as recovered money, an approved refund, or a universally valid charge. Do not invent a dollar amount or savings percent.

### Voice direction

Confident American male conversational delivery, warm and serious, not aggressive, shouted, synthetic, growled or overly deep. Spoken pronunciation **“re-tally”**; for a TTS engine use pronunciation override `ree-TAL-ee` on the company's name, but leave the exact written spelling `RETALLY` everywhere. Listen at least twice to each generated take and approve *the actual pronunciation*, not phonetic prompt text. This is brand-critical.

### Visual production rules

1. Use a legitimately licensed original presenter or clearly authorized AI presenter **with credible lip synchronization**, not an unlicensed face lifted from the reference Reel. If unavailable, use an evidence-first video without faking an on-camera speaker; call that a compromise, not reference matched.
2. Camera should retain one consistent studio lighting setup and wardrobe across the short. Portrait framed and iPhone-safe. Presenter occupies enough pixels to read the expression and feel real.
3. Freight b-roll must depict actual motion (not three still photographs with Ken Burns zoom). Avoid repeated stock images, plastic AI hands, impossible trucks, distorted text on screens and legible customer identifiers.
4. All document numbers and timeline elements are typed in an editor as sharp graphics, not rendered by generative video. Grade the footage, composite the evidence, then add titles: this prevents nonsense text.
5. Palette from the approved RETALLY brand: midnight `#0E1E29`, emerald `#05B873`, ink `#576D71`, white. Gloss is limited to the approved master logo; never manufacture a 3-D fake logo.
6. Use the ORIGINAL approved wordmark asset `freight/site/assets/brand/retally-wordmark-approved.png` with a white plate and >= 0.3× artwork-height clear space. Do not put the redundant standalone folded-R logo next to the wordmark.
7. `ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE — NOT ACTUAL CUSTOMER RESULTS` is visible whenever the example is being presented, but sized so it does not compete with the main information.
8. Professional captions timed to speech, at most one short phrase at a time. Check 0:05, 0:12, 0:19, 0:26 manually on a small-screen preview.
9. Narration at full, consistent level with gentle compression, tasteful instrumentals at least ~15dB below it, true peak under -1 dBTP, no clipping, fade out naturally. Prefer owned/commercially cleared music with recorded license/provenance; no music whose off-platform license is unknown.
10. No invented real client, fee, guaranteed refund, real case number, recovered amount, commercial certification, or secure-upload capability. The site currently prepares an email draft instead of a fully active online intake workflow.
