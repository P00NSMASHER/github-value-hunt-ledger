## Registration timing caveat

Pennsylvania's official guidance generally requires registration when commercial activity is conducted under a name other than the proper legal name. Fictitious-name registration and LLC formation are different; fictitious-name registration does not create liability protection. Therefore, absence of a paying customer is not a blanket registration exemption, and the existing prelaunch activities and selected structure need appropriate legal review. The instruction not to file now is preserved; waiting until payment is not certified as legally sufficient.

Source: https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/programs/business/types-of-filings-and-registrations/fictitious-names
