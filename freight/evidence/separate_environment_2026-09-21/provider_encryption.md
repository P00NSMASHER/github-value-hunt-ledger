# Provider encryption evidence — Google Drive

Observed: 2026-09-21

Google Drive Help states that files uploaded to Drive are encrypted in transit and at rest.
Source: https://support.google.com/drive/answer/10375054

A second Google Drive Help page states that files uploaded to Drive or created in Docs, Sheets, and Slides are encrypted in transit and at rest with AES-256.
Source: https://support.google.com/drive/answer/10519333

This evidence supports the provider-level encryption-at-rest and encryption-in-transit controls for the staged Google Drive workspace. It does not prove account-specific MFA.
