# RETALLY — four-document same-shipment audit (19 October 2026)

**No fully independent four-document freight recovery chain was found. No contacts, purchases, logins, FOIA submissions or original third-party source bytes published.** This report separates independently documented payment evidence, rate terms, adjudications, and *actual realized recoveries*. The user attests to download rights. This is not automatic authorization to redistribute third-party bank statements or unpublished rate contracts.

## Exact chain required

For one *same* carrier, payer, invoice and shipment/BOL/container, verify:
1. **Original issued underlying carrier freight invoice** and shipment identity (not merely a broker-issued invoice or a litigant's description of a bill).
2. **Effective controlling written freight rate** on that shipment's service date, including line/accessorial basis. An invoice's self-stated $/mile or list tariff number is NOT itself conclusive rate authority.
3. **Independent initial payment** with bank/processor clearing, amount and original invoice allocation (not payment instructions, "paid" ledger status, a check mailed, a sworn assertion, an authorization or an unallocated remittance batch).
4. **Actual executed freight recovery** linked to that *same invoice* (independent credit memo applied, Treasury offset settlement, bank-cleared refund). A judgment award, petition to deposit, uncashed refund check, possible credit or liability transfer is NOT actual settled recovery. Independently adjudicated rating error is required additionally for validation labels.

## Strongest newly located sources

### A. FMC 22-18 — Color Brands LLC vs AAF Logistics Inc (highest original-invoice + ACH lead, but not realized recovery)

- [Original sworn declaration with exhibits](https://www2.fmc.gov/readingroom/docs/22-18/22-18%20Declaration%20with%20Exhibits%20of%20Daniel%20Lutz%20in%20Support%20of%20Complainant%27s%20Request%20for%20Judgment.pdf/) describes copies of the original AAF-issued ocean freight/documentation/cargo-insurance invoices and **redacted ACH-payment records** for **I1909095 (Sep 9, 2019), I2007047 (Jul 16, 2020), I2109072 (Sep 14, 2021), I2201018 (Jan 16, 2022)**. The 2021 and 2022 examples also have a batch-payment allocation email connecting the invoice reference to the ACH payment. This is better than an invoice plus a "remit to" instruction.
- [FMC 2024 independent final administrative adjudication](https://www.fmc.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/8-F.M.C.2d.pdf) in Docket **22-18** found AAF defaulted, established violations concerning cargo-insurance premiums and mishandled cargo claims, and awarded reparations; administratively final Sep 27, 2024. **A court order to pay is not evidence that AAF actually paid Color Brands.**
- Original invoices concern **ocean freight and cargo insurance**, not a carrier LTL rate overcharge. The litigant states insurance was charged at an average reported percentage of cargo value, but no executed **effective transportation-rate contract** for each identified shipment was independently matched.
- **PROVEN by public searchable filed text:** invoices and redacted ACH evidence are described as exhibited. **NOT directly replayed from source PDF bytes in this run:** FMC source download returned source-unavailable in the read-only GitHub Actions job; contents, bank clearing status and exact invoice-level matching remain to be independently inspected privately. **MISSING:** separately bank-cleared final award/refund and valid freight-rate error label. An award for premiums/insurance cannot be relabeled a freight-rating overcharge.

### B. FMC 23-01 — Samsung Electronics America (SEA) vs SM Line Corp (critical unrecovered-award control)

- [Public FMC docket](https://www2.fmc.gov/readingroom/proceeding/23-01/) includes paid detention/demurrage invoice disputes and a final independent merits decision. [Sep 29, 2026 order on petition to deposit funds](https://www2.fmc.gov/readingroom/docs/23-01/%28161%29%2023-01%20Order%20on%20Petition%20to%20Deposit%20Funds%20%28public%29.pdf/) confirmed **115 liability findings**, **$1,913,355.80 reparations awarded** in Aug 2026, and efforts by SM Line to tender about **$2.3 million including interest**.
- **Final agency finding:** SEA **refused** the attempted payment; SM Line instead claimed to have transferred the amount to its *own counsel's IOLA trust account*. The FMC **denied** SM Line's petition to treat that deposit as satisfaction of its obligation or to accept funds into an agency account. **This is not a bank-cleared recovery to SEA and cannot be counted as customer cash recovered**.
- The underlying record includes more than 17,000 contested charges; the public ruling upholds only specified categories. Do not automatically count all D&D charges as overbilled. The original invoices, effective rate terms and independent individual bank receipts were not joined here into one identified paid-and-refunded shipment.

### C. FMC 26-10 — Kaushik vs SFL Worldwide (original quote and original payer transfer leads, no executed refund)

Includes source invoice/quote, shipping correspondence, BOL and carrier delivery-ticket allegations.
- A post-complaint **$750 settlement offer was conditional on a signed release**; an offer is not an executed or accepted refund. Original Zelle records described in the exhibit do not prove bank settlement to the final transport service principal without independent validation; the dispute is not final independently adjudicated overcharge truth.
- FMC documents were not downloaded by GitHub's public source reader (origin unavailable), so treat details as official **source-indexed text**, not as a locally hashed and source-page-replayed bundle.
