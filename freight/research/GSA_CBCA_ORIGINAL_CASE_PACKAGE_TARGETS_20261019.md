## Original-document custody and access evidence

- [FAR 47.103-1(c)-(e), Acquisition.gov](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/47.103-1) prescribes forwarding **original copies of paid freight bills/invoices, bills of lading and supporting documents** in one postpayment audit package to GSA *where the specified FAR cost-reimbursement clause applies*; it also provides supporting vouchers/check numbers. **Not a public download or proof a named 2018 file is still retained.**
- [41 CFR 102-118.425 (updated Nov. 2024)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/41/102-118.425) specifies TAMS submission metadata including invoice, payment amount/date, voucher and complete tender/tariff authority. [2024 Federal Register rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-11-26/pdf/2024-27552.pdf) explains that agencies now submit electronic records to central TAMS repository; paper address in older FAR is not the modern access workflow.
- **Procedural correction:** [48 CFR Part 6103](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/48/part-6103) separately governs **transportation RATE cases**; the CBCA [Rule 4 appeal-file procedure](https://www.cbca.gov/howto/rules/Rule4Appeal.html) concerns other types of contract disputes and **must not be assumed to require an exhibit index in RATE proceedings**. Ask for existing RATE-case filings, attachments/exhibits and any docket index *if one exists*. Some materials may be protected or absent.
- [CBCA Clerk contact and 2026 RATE-case rules](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/48/6103.301) identify the appropriate source-record inquiry route. [CBCA Rule 9(e)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/48/6101.9) pertains to contract disputes and **should not be assumed automatically applicable to transportation RATE-case files**. Free copies or public access to specific RATE-case exhibits are **not guaranteed**. [CBCA Clerk information](https://www.cbca.gov/howto/Howtofile.html) supplies proper *records-inquiry* pathway, not authorization to efile or contact without owner approval.
- [GSA FOIA portal and guidance](https://www.gsa.gov/reference/freedom-of-information-act-foia) permit requests for existing agency files, not freshly constructed reconciliations. Public reading-room search **did not expose the matching complete original-bill, effective-tariff, original paid/offset voucher bundle**. Login.gov may be required to submit or download records; commercial FOIA requests may incur fees absent explicit waiver, so user has authorized **$0 spending** only.
- Government-authored **decisions** can be used as historical legal narratives with attribution. Carrier-authored invoice scans, BOLs, private rate contracts, bank statements and non-public exhibits **are not deemed public-domain or commercially reusable** merely because held by GSA or filed before CBCA.
