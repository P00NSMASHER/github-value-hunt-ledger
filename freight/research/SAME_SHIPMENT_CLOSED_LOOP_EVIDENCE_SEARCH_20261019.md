The objective is strict: all independently verifiable originals must link using the *same* original carrier, payer/legal entity, invoice number or PRO/BOL/container, service date and money-movement references. Similar charge totals or company names are insufficient. An adjudicator's statement that an offset was collected is legally meaningful historical evidence but **not an independently inspected original bank clearing record**. A customer reporting receipt of a paper check is not proof it cleared the issuing account.

## Audit findings and separations

1. **Invoice numbers, BOLs and carrier references are case identifiers, not independent paid evidence.** The original Pro Transport invoice clearly refers to the specific container and BOL; the invoiced **$1.20/mile** is not automatically the governing contracted rate.
2. **Correct rate ≠ court win ≠ executed recovery ≠ bank settlement.** The Western Truck Lines case identifies all four events *in a single federal adjudicator's narrative* except the unproduced source original documents; that does **not** establish independent 4-way source verification.
3. **A received refund check does not establish an admitted overcharge.** The ABF July 2026 correspondence reports a check received, but ABF claims it was a goodwill payment, and the legal/rating truth is contested. No bank-cleared check, invoice PDF or controlling rate.
4. **A confidential mediated settlement is not a disclosed $ amount, executed refund or bank evidence.** The Pro Transport–Seaboard case's private Florida mediation settlement cannot be used as source label.
5. **The goal remains same legal payer, shipment and event lineage.** Do not cross-join the ABF bankruptcy invoice with an unrelated ABF BBB refund or join GlobalTranz's XPO reference to another debtor's carrier proof of claim.
