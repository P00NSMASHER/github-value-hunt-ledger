# UNSENT DRAFT — targeted GSA TAMS original-source records request
**Prepared October 19, 2026; NOT SENT.** This is a preliminary public-record request template, not a filed FOIA request, a commercial license, a waived fee, or an assumption that GSA can release third-party carrier records.

**Authoritative request portal:** https://securefoia.gsa.gov/app/Home.aspx . Public guidance: https://www.gsa.gov/reference/freedom-of-information-act-foia and https://www.gsa.gov/reference/freedom-of-information-act-foia/foia-library

## Subject
FOIA — Existing redacted, closed GSA freight post-payment audit case files, original carrier invoices, effective rate authorities, paid vouchers, and final executed refunds/offsets (2025–2026)

## Proposed request text

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, I request electronic copies of up to **ten existing, closed, postpayment motor-freight/LTL transportation-overcharge audit case files** administered by GSA Transportation Audits Division during January 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026, if such a retrievable subset exists in the Transportation Audits Management System (TAMS) or its ordinary records systems. Please limit the responsive set to cases in which final liability was resolved **and an actual refund or offset was recorded as executed**, if records are searchable on that basis. If this selection would require the creation of a new analysis or data extract, please advise what narrower existing index or disposition file can be produced without creating new records. No new legal analysis, computations, or generated summary is requested.

For each responsive existing case file, please include, to the extent retained and releasable:

1. Original carrier-issued invoice or transportation bill (including invoice number, line items, invoice date and billed amount), carrier SCAC, applicable bill of lading/PRO/DRN and proof of performed transportation.
2. Original or identified governing tariff, tender, quotation, or contract rate schedule, including the section/item and **effective date at shipment**. Private rate contracts should be reviewed for applicable FOIA exemptions and third-party rights.
3. Original agency payment voucher and transportation payment record, reflecting voucher identifier, actual date and amount paid and its corresponding original carrier invoice. If separate releasable evidence of Treasury settlement/ACH/check clearing exists, please include it; do not infer that ordinary authorization records prove clearing.
4. Issued Statement of Difference, Notice of Overcharge (Form 7925 or successor), carrier response and the final, independently signed GSA/GAO/CBCA administrative decision, settlement certificate or other adjudication for the same invoice or shipment.
5. Executed refund/offset transaction receipt, reconciliation ledger event, Treasury credit or check clearing record showing the exact outcome, date and linkage to the disputed payment/invoice. **An award or unexecuted instruction is not sufficient** for this field.
6. Index/document register fields sufficient to match these records by invoice, BOL/PRO/DRN and case identity without relying on supplier names or sensitive banking details.

**Redactions / minimization.** Please remove employee personal details, banking numbers, carrier tax identifiers, personal addresses, private account numbers, passwords, and material subject to valid FOIA exemptions. I do not seek confidential proprietary business information that cannot lawfully be released. A segregable case with releaseable documents is preferable to withheld confidential material. Please provide a redaction/exemption explanation for missing evidence components where practicable.

**Format and provenance.** Please provide available original PDFs and machine-readable pre-existing indexes (CSV/XLSX), existing per-file document identifiers, creation/modified dates and the governing FOIA release notice. Do not create new source records or synthesize omitted invoices. I will independently hash exact received bytes, document original provenance, and perform separate rights review.

**Access and rights.** This request does not ask GSA to grant intellectual-property rights it does not own. If any records remain third-party copyrighted, please identify that constraint where available. I seek a factual record of a publicly documented historical transportation overcharge for reproducible accounting-method research; any commercial testing/redistribution would undergo independent authority and permission review.

**Fees.** I will not authorize chargeable processing or acquisition. If any search, duplication, review, or other fee is anticipated, please notify me with a cost estimate before proceeding. Please do not incur fees without express authorization. I am open to a much narrower already-released zero-cost subset instead.
