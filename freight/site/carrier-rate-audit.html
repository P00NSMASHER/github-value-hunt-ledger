Carrier rate audit

A correct calculation can still use the wrong rate.

Carrier-rate review must resolve which contract, tariff, rate card, lane rule, amendment, and effective date controlled each shipment before comparing billed and expected charges.

On this pageOverviewStart free audit
01

Rate authority comes first

Before rerating an invoice, identify the controlling contract, rate confirmation, tariff, spot quote, amendment, or customer-specific schedule. Missing authority should not be replaced with a default rate.

02

Common rate problems

Typical issues include stale rate versions, wrong lanes, service mismatches, minimum-charge errors, incorrect weight breaks, incorrect dimensional or volumetric basis, and discounts or surcharges applied outside their effective window.

03

Fuel and accessorial interaction

Base freight, fuel, and accessorial rules may use different formulas, effective dates, caps, or rounding conventions. Audit each component according to the source that governs it rather than forcing a single generic formula.

04

Evidence for a rate dispute

A defensible case links the shipment, billed amount, controlling rate source, calculation steps, source-effective date, and review decision. The result should be reproducible by someone who did not perform the original audit.
