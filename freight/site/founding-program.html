01

What founding customers receive

  • $0 bounded historical freight audit.
  • Priority review queue.
  • No subscription, implementation fee, or per-shipment fee.
  • Recovery Evidence Pack for validated findings.
  • Second-Look Recovery Audit when existing TMS/audit/payment controls already exist.
  • Customer authorization before carrier or outside recovery action.
  • Discounted contingency terms relative to the then-current standard rate, confirmed in writing before recovery work.
  • No recovery fee when eligible actual recovered funds are $0.
02

Who is a strong fit

The program is aimed at U.S. distributors and multi-site shippers with meaningful LTL or parcel activity, multiple locations or carriers, internal AP/finance, at least six months of usable history, and commercial authority sufficient to test billed charges.

Priority sectors include HVACR, plumbing/PVF, electrical, building materials, industrial/MRO, fasteners, packaging, safety/JanSan, plastics, welding/gases, and food distribution.

03

The objective is real evidence, not launch theater

We want to move from real records to supported findings, customer-authorized recovery actions, settlements, reversals, and verified actual recovery. A defensible no-recovery result is also useful evidence about the methodology.
