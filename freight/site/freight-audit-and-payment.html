01

What freight audit and payment covers

A freight audit and payment process verifies that carrier invoices correspond to real shipments, valid commercial terms, and the services actually performed before or after payment. It should catch duplicate billing, wrong rate versions, incorrect fuel or accessorial application, unsupported charges, and mismatches between approved and settled amounts.

02

Why finance and logistics both matter

Transportation teams understand the shipment and carrier context; AP and finance teams control invoice approval, credits, and settlement. A defensible audit joins those views instead of treating the freight bill as a standalone accounting document.

03

Pre-pay versus post-pay review

Pre-pay controls stop unsupported charges before payment. Post-pay recovery reviews historical populations for billing errors that were already paid. The same evidence principles apply: controlling rates, shipment facts, a reproducible expected charge, and clear settlement attribution.

04

What to measure

Useful metrics include invoices reviewed, findings accepted, false-positive rate, carrier response, issued credits, actual recovered funds, reversals, review time, and net contribution after recovery effort. Estimated savings should remain separate from realized recovery.
