01

Freight invoice errors worth testing

High-value review categories include exact or near-duplicate billing, wrong rate versions, incorrect minimums, unsupported accessorials, quantity or weight mismatches, detention timing errors, incorrect fuel or surcharge application, currency problems and credits that never reach the final account state.

The important control is evidence. A difference should remain a review item until the controlling source and shipment facts support it.

02

Use invoice data plus controlling records

Invoice exports are only one plane. Depending on the carrier and mode, review may also need contracts, rate confirmations, tariffs, shipment events, BOL/POD records, appointment or dwell evidence and later credit or payment records. Missing authority is not treated as a zero rate.

03

A defensible invoice-audit workflow

Freeze the invoice population, identify the controlling source, calculate the expected charge independently, compare actual billing, route ambiguous cases to review and keep later settlement separate from the original finding. That sequence prevents one attractive discrepancy from quietly becoming a fake recovery number.

04

What an invoice audit cannot prove by itself

An invoice discrepancy is not the same as an approved claim or recovered cash. Existing carrier credits, incumbent-known findings, contractual exceptions, missing documents and later reversals all affect what can legitimately be called recovered.
