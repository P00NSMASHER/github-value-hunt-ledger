Questions

Plain answers.

Clear scope. Checkable findings. No hidden automation.

01 What records could a review need?

Invoices, governing rates or contracts, amendments, and relevant shipment or accessorial evidence. A POD or BOL alone may not prove the right charge.

02 Does a flag mean a refund is owed?

No. A flag may need more evidence or interpretation. We keep reviewed, validated, and realized amounts separate.

03 Will you automatically dispute invoices?

No. Your authorized reviewer decides. Any outside action needs separate, specific approval.

04 Are the demo results from a customer?

No. Every identity, record, approval, money event, and amount is synthetic. The demo shows workflow behavior, not customer value.

05 How do I share freight documents?

Not through this page or the first inquiry. Actual records move only after scope, rights, access, retention, and a controlled intake route are agreed.
