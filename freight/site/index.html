Straight answers

What finance and freight teams usually ask.

Conservative answers now prevent expensive misunderstandings later.

01How much does the audit cost?

The initial recovery audit is $0 upfront. It is designed to determine whether a meaningful recovery opportunity appears to exist and what the next step should be.

02What happens if you find nothing?

You owe no audit fee. If no viable opportunity is identified, there is no recovery engagement and no recovery fee.

03How does Freight Recovery get paid?

If you separately authorize a recovery engagement, Freight Recovery earns the agreed percentage of eligible funds actually recovered. A potential opportunity, flag, or unpaid claim does not by itself earn a fee.

04What percentage do you charge?

The current working rate is confirmed before recovery work begins. It is a configurable commercial term, not a permanent promise. Your exact rate, scope, exclusions, and payment timing are confirmed in writing before the engagement starts.

05When do I owe a fee?

Only after an authorized engagement produces fee-eligible funds actually received or posted to you, subject to the signed terms. Known reversals reduce the eligible amount. If actual eligible recovery is zero, the recovery fee is zero.

06What information do you need?

Qualification starts with freight-spend and shipment ranges, modes, carrier count, available history, approximate invoice volume, record types, and whether issues or prior audits are known. Freight files are requested only after scope and a secure transfer route are approved.

07What freight types can you analyze?

The qualification flow accepts parcel, LTL, FTL, intermodal, ocean, air, and mixed-mode descriptions. Actual support depends on the available invoices, rate authority, shipment evidence, payment evidence, carrier, geography, and agreed scope. We confirm fit before accepting records.

08How far back can you review?

Available history, contractual deadlines, carrier rules, limitation periods, record quality, and the agreed scope all matter. Share the history range during qualification; we confirm the review period before analysis. This is not legal advice.

09What happens after you identify a recovery?

You receive a concise opportunity summary. If you want Freight Recovery to proceed, we define the cases, authority, contingency rate, recovered-funds definition, data handling, and payment terms in a separate engagement before detailed claim preparation or outside contact.

10Can we perform the recovery ourselves?

You control whether to proceed. The free audit summary supports that decision, but it does not include the full proprietary claim package, detailed execution strategy, carrier correspondence, or operational playbook reserved for the recovery engagement.

11Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

Not to request the free audit. Any recovery engagement should define its exact scope, term, termination rights, and treatment of in-process or finding-originated recoveries. No long-term commitment is implied by this site.

12How is our freight data protected?

The public form does not accept freight files. We first confirm fit, scope, allowed records, and an approved transfer route. Access and retention requirements are then documented for the engagement. Do not email invoices, credentials, contracts, or payment data.

13What if we prefer a fixed-fee audit?

A fixed-fee forensic audit may be available by request for organizations that want to fund the analysis directly and retain all eligible recoveries. It is custom scoped and priced in writing; it is not the primary launch offer.
