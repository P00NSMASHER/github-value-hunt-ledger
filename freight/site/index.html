Questions

Clear scope. Checkable findings.

What records can an audit require?

Carrier invoices, the governing rate confirmation or contract, and the shipment or accessorial evidence needed for the selected checks.

Does a finding mean the carrier owes money?

No. A validated discrepancy means the charge differs from the supported amount under the reviewed evidence. Recovery is counted only from later settlement evidence.

Will Freight Recovery automatically contact carriers?

No. Buyer review and a separate, narrowly scoped authorization are required before any carrier-facing action.

What if the audit finds nothing?

A defensible clean result is a valid outcome. The report still accounts for the reviewed population and evidence coverage.

Are the examples customer results?

No. All amounts on this page are synthetic.
