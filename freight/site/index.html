A focused first engagement

Agree the review before moving documents.

Begin with the freight mix, invoice volume, and the decision you want to improve. Then define the evidence, review scope, and handling arrangements together.

1

Check fit and readiness

Identify the relevant rate sources, invoice exports, and delivery records. Confirm a suitable, controlled intake route before sharing actual freight documents.

2

Review the evidence

Compare billed charges against applicable terms. Record missing evidence, disputed interpretations, and supported exceptions separately.

3

Measure the outcome

Track review effort and resolved exceptions. Count a recovery or avoided payment only when the agreed evidence supports it.

What would a useful pilot answer?

Define the problem and the decision.

A short fit discussion can establish whether the available records can support a meaningful review.

  • Freight modes and approximate invoice volume
  • Access to governing rates and supporting records
  • Review bottlenecks and the outcome to measure
  • A named reviewer and decision maker

Questions

Clear scope. Checkable findings.

What records could a review require?

Carrier invoices, the governing rate confirmation or contract, and relevant delivery or accessorial evidence. A POD or BOL alone may not establish the right to a particular charge.

Does a flag mean the carrier owes a refund?

No. A flag may require more evidence or interpretation. Confirmed recoveries and avoided payments are tracked separately from potential variances.

Will you automatically reject or dispute invoices?

No. Your authorized reviewer makes payment and dispute decisions. Any external action requires separate authorization.

Are the sample results from a customer?

No. Every amount shown here is synthetic and illustrates a possible review format.

How do I share freight documents?

Do not upload them to this page or attach them to the initial inquiry. A pilot begins only after scope, access, retention, and a suitable controlled intake route are agreed and verified.

What happens when I prepare an inquiry?

The page builds a text draft locally. You can copy it or download it. Once a verified business contact is configured, you can open an email draft and send it yourself. The page cannot confirm delivery.
