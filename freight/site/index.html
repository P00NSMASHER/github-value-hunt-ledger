Cancel for a full fixed-fee refund before written scope confirmation. Once scope is confirmed and work begins, the fixed fee is non-refundable except where required by law or agreed in writing.

Managed recovery is optional and separately authorized. The fee is 20% of uniquely attributable, net realized amounts. It is invoiced after the customer receives or is credited the money.

If a credited amount is later reversed, the eligible recovery and fee are recalculated. Any resulting fee credit or refund is applied to the customer.

Questions

Plain answers.

Clear prices. Checkable findings. No hidden charges.

01 What does the fixed fee pay for?

The selected readiness review or freight audit, human review, and written deliverables shown above. It does not include ongoing monitoring, carrier disputes, or managed recovery.

02 When is the 20% recovery fee charged?

Only after separately authorized work produces an eligible credit, refund, or remittance that the customer actually receives or is credited. A finding or claim by itself earns no recovery fee.

03 How much of a recovery does the customer keep?

The customer keeps 80% of each fee-eligible net realized recovery. Freight Recovery earns 20%. The one-time fixed service fee is separate.

04 What amounts are excluded?

Flags, unpaid claims, unsupported or unresolved amounts, duplicates, incumbent-known findings, automatic or preexisting credits, and reversals are not fee-eligible.

05 Does checkout authorize a dispute or data upload?

No. Your authorized reviewer decides whether any outside action occurs. Actual records move only after scope, rights, retention, and a controlled intake route are confirmed.

06 Are the demo results from a customer?

No. Every identity, record, approval, money event, and amount is synthetic. The demo shows workflow behavior, not customer value.
