02

How this calculation works

NMFTA's published handling-unit method uses the greatest straight-line length × width × height in inches, including projections, divided by 1,728 to obtain cubic feet. Gross handling-unit weight ÷ volume gives density in pounds per cubic foot (PCF). This comparison multiplies both weight and cube by the number of identical units and uses full-precision values before displaying two decimals.

Source: NMFTA, How is the density of a handling unit calculated? (March 2026). NMFC modernization took effect July 19, 2025; classification still requires the applicable item, customer/carrier commercial terms and potentially handling, stowability or liability factors. See NMFTA's classification FAQ.

Illustration, not a freight-class quote.

A pallet measuring 48 × 40 × 45 inches and weighing 450 lb has 50 cubic feet and 9.00 PCF. A revised 48 × 40 × 48 inch, 500 lb measurement is 53.33 cubic feet and 9.375 PCF. Those figures describe the proposed measurement change; they do not establish whether it was authorized or incorrectly charged.

04

Freight density and reweigh FAQ

Does a higher density mean the carrier's reclassification is correct?

Not automatically. Freight class also depends on the controlling NMFC item and sub-number, agreed tariff or exceptions, and potentially handling, stowability or liability. Weight and volume alone are not a class determination.

Is a higher revised gross weight proof of an overcharge?

No. Confirm the actual measurement, date, contract, effective rating rule and any corrected invoices or credits. A numerical discrepancy alone is not an allowed refund.

Can this tool calculate different pallet sizes in one shipment?

It compares one identical handling-unit type at a time. Mixed types need separately measured handling units; never apply one unit's dimensions across dissimilar pallets.

Are my invoices or measurements uploaded?

No freight records or personal details are requested. This comparator uses local arithmetic on entered numbers, without writing them to a RETALLY server or local browser storage. Other website features such as optional chat are separate.
