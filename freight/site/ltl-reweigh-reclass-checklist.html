01

Weight adjustment and freight reclassification are different questions

Reweigh: the carrier reports a different measured weight. Reclassification: the carrier applies a different freight class or rating basis. Both can affect a bill, but neither is automatically incorrect.

The original bill of lading is an important source, not necessarily the final governing measurement or classification. Resolve the accepted agreement, effective tariff, agreed freight-all-kinds (FAK) exception, commodity attributes and actual measurement evidence.

02

The seven-source review packet

  1. Shipment/pro identity, date, carrier and origin/destination.
  2. Original tender and BOL description, pieces, stated weight and dimensions.
  3. Applicable customer rate agreement, classification, tariff, effective date, FAK or exceptions.
  4. Carrier's original and revised weight/class and basis for its change.
  5. Supporting scale measurement, inspection or dimensions, as applicable.
  6. Before/after invoice calculations, discounts, minimums and fuel treatment.
  7. Related credits, rebills, prior disputes, earlier audit findings and settlements.

05

Buyer-authorized review questions for a carrier

  • What dated inspection or measurement supports the revised weight or class?
  • Which governing rate and classification rule applied at the time?
  • What is the exact original versus revised charge calculation?
  • Were any related credits or corrected invoices already applied?

Carrier contact, formal disputes and document transfer require separate buyer authority. Do not infer carrier liability from absent information alone.

06

LTL reweigh and reclass FAQ

Is a reweigh fee automatically an overcharge?

No. Verify the measurement, governing rate terms, adjustment authority and actual recalculation before reaching a conclusion.

What if the customer negotiated a freight-all-kinds classification exception?

Check the actual FAK language, effective dates and covered shipment scope before applying a general class rating.

Can we upload a BOL or tariff here?

No. Discuss non-sensitive scope first; confidential documents require a separately authorized secure route.
