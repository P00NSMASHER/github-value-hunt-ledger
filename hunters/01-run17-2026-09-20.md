### Concrete capability
Familiarise is the first Node 01 find in this sequence that materially joins both halves of the target:

1. **Event-time economic authority.** Revenue split is resolved using the rate card effective at `payment.createdAt`; the source explicitly warns that using the live/current time would let a retroactive rate bump rewrite what the consultant was owed. Earnings persist the applied rate-card identity/basis-point snapshot and resulting integer-money shares.
2. **Durable earning artifact.** Consultant/org earnings hold the calculated share amounts and applied split data; payout batching later sums those stored earning amounts rather than asking the current rate card to price the work again.
3. **One-use payout execution.** READY earnings are atomically claimed into BATCHED and linked to a payout; APPROVED → PROCESSING is a CAS claim so only one runner may touch the gateway. If the gateway accepted but local persistence fails, the payout is quarantined PROCESSING with provider identity and the earnings remain linked rather than being freed for a duplicate send.
4. **External post-success counter-event.** Razorpay `payout.reversed` is routed through the webhook dispatcher to `markConsultantPayoutReversed(providerPayoutId, reason)`.
5. **Exact prior-payout identity and revocable finality.** The reversal function resolves the exact payout by provider payout ID and only claims `COMPLETED → REVERSED`. A redelivery/no-longer-COMPLETED row becomes a no-op.
6. **Compensating accounting + reopened obligation.** The function posts the exact inverse payout journal using a distinct idempotency key, converts linked PAID earnings back to READY, clears their payout linkage/paid timestamp, and leaves them eligible for a future payout batch.
7. **Original-authority re-payment by composition.** Because the future batch sums the previously persisted earning-share amounts and does not re-resolve the current rate card, the source path implies that a re-payment after reversal preserves the original earning amount/rate authority rather than pricing the event under a later rate card.

### Why unusual
Most payout systems found in this lane fail at one of two boundaries: they preserve historical earning economics but do not reopen after a late external return, or they handle payout reversal but only while the earning is still pending/scheduled and cannot reopen a previously paid obligation. Familiarise connects event-time rate authority, persisted earning artifacts, post-success bank/provider reversal, compensating journal, reopened payable, and future re-batching in one system.

### Strongest objection / red-team result
The remaining material weakness is **late-event observation redundancy**. Repository runbook/docs state the gateway poller still re-polls only PENDING/PROCESSING payouts. The post-COMPLETED reversal path therefore depends on receiving the `payout.reversed` webhook; a permanently lost reversal webhook is not independently rediscovered by the ordinary payout poller. This prevents claiming complete bank-finality assurance from Familiarise alone.

A second limitation: the broader refund-driven clawback against an already-paid payout is still described as a manual recovery path; the automated strength here is specifically bank/provider payout reversal.

## CLAIMS TESTED

1. **Can one executable vertical system combine event-time rate authority and a later externally observed post-success payout reversal?**
   - **SUPPORTED.** Familiarise resolves earnings under payment-time rate authority and routes external `payout.reversed` into a post-COMPLETED compensating reversal.

2. **Does the late reversal reopen the underlying economic obligation rather than merely changing provider status?**
   - **SUPPORTED.** PAID earnings are restored to READY, original payout linkage/paid timestamp removed, and inverse payable/cash/TDS accounting posted.

3. **Can the same late event be applied twice?**
   - **GUARDED.** Only a COMPLETED payout can be claimed into REVERSED; later redelivery is a no-op. The reversal journal has a distinct stable idempotency key. Initial payout execution is also CAS-claimed before gateway submission.

4. **Will a repayment after reversal use current rates?**
   - **SOURCE-IMPLIED NO.** The earning amount/split was already calculated and persisted under `payment.createdAt`; re-batching sums the reopened earning artifact rather than resolving the current rate card. A dedicated end-to-end test that changes the rate between original payout and re-payment would further strengthen this claim.

5. **Is late reversal guaranteed to be discovered if a webhook is lost?**
   - **NO.** This remains the largest gap. The ordinary poller covers PENDING/PROCESSING, not already-COMPLETED payouts.

## EMERGING TECHNOLOGY SIGNALS

This materially strengthens **authority-aware revocable financial finality** as an emerging reusable architecture:

`event-time authority → durable earning → one-use effect identity → provider success → non-terminal finality → explicit counter-event → compensating entry → reopened original obligation → re-close`

The important capability is not a `REVERSED` enum. It is the conjunction of historical pricing authority, exact external identity, irreversible-history/compensating accounting, idempotent reopen, and re-payment from the original economic artifact.

## FAILURES / NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE

- A payout reversal handler that filters only `SCHEDULED`, `PENDING`, or `payout_pending` is structurally unable to repair **success → later return**. Both Connect Laundry and Superherocpr exhibit this bug class.
- Having a frozen amount/rate snapshot does not guarantee revocable finality; the state machine must explicitly reopen a previously PAID obligation.
- Having `payout.reversed` support does not guarantee historical entitlement preservation; the re-payment path must consume the original earning artifact rather than re-price from current policy.
- Webhook-only late-finality observation is still not independent bank/provider finality. A COMPLETED payout needs either long-tail provider polling or a bank/settlement observer capable of identifying later returns.
- Shadow/inert launch architecture should not be promoted as executable money movement simply because its schema is sophisticated.
