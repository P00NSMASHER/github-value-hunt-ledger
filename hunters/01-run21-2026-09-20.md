This is not a generic payroll CRUD add-on. It contains executable South African statutory-domain logic and workflows across PAYE, UIF, SDL, ETI, medical credits, retirement deductions, payroll EFT batches, EMP201 monthly declarations, EMP501 reconciliation, IRP5/IT3(a) certificates, VAT201 working papers, labour/skills records, employment-equity reporting support, COIDA annual returns, workplace injuries and OID claims.

The valuable asset is domain compression: an ERPNext deployment gains a large body of country-specific payroll/tax/labour workflow and evidence handling that would otherwise require substantial practitioner discovery, data modeling, rate maintenance, report construction and test design.

### Commercial thesis
**Buyer:** South African ERPNext implementers, payroll bureaus, accounting firms, and SMEs already using or evaluating ERPNext/HRMS.

**Painful problem:** Local statutory payroll and tax compliance is not merely a tax table. It requires date-effective payroll rules, SARS code mappings, monthly/annual reconciliations, payroll-to-GL integrity, local bank files, certificate/report surfaces, and evidence that current rules did not rewrite historic periods.

**First paid wedge:** `ERPNext South Africa Payroll Compliance Pack + Readiness Audit` — deploy the localization into a customer's staging ERPNext/HRMS instance, ingest/configure their payroll components and mappings, run deterministic 2026/27 compliance fixtures, reconcile one historical month, and produce a practitioner-review exception pack before production cutover.

**Money path:** implementation fees + annual statutory-rate/update subscription + payroll-close/reconciliation assurance + practitioner/ERP partner licensing/support.

**Build/domain advantage:** materially compresses months of local payroll/tax/labour discovery and implementation. It supplies executable rate packs, mappings, workflows, reports and tests instead of only documentation.

### Strongest objections / red-team
- Direct SARS/e@syFile/eFiling submission is explicitly absent. This is a working-paper and ERP processing layer, not a complete statutory transmission product.
- Only one tested payroll EFT bank format (FNB Enterprise CSV) is supported; other banks require separate onboarding/specification rights and acceptance testing.
- The app depends on Frappe/ERPNext v16 and HRMS v16 for payroll, narrowing the addressable installed base.
- Statutory correctness is time-sensitive. Rate packs need continuing authoritative review; CI success is software evidence, not tax-practitioner signoff.
- The value is regional and compliance-heavy, so distribution likely works best through local ERP/accounting partners rather than broad self-serve SaaS.
