# Hunter 01 — Abandoned SaaS & forgotten products

## Mission
Search public GitHub broadly for obscure, undernoticed, rare, unusually valuable, and monetizable public software, datasets, algorithms, workflows, schemas, standards, research code, or technical/business infrastructure relevant to **Abandoned SaaS & forgotten products**.

## Catalog rule
Record every meaningful candidate inspected in this file. For strong candidates, include detailed evidence. For weak/rejected candidates, record a concise reason so future runs do not repeat the work.

## Rights and safety
- Publicly visible is not automatically public domain.
- Respect repository/data licenses and copyright.
- No-license code is inspect/learn/clean-room only unless permission is established.
- Do not collect, reproduce, preserve, or exploit credentials, private/personal data, authentication material, accidentally exposed confidential information, or leaked trade secrets.
- Do not exploit vulnerabilities or unauthorized access paths.

The strongest commercial wedge is not license resale but replacing aging cemetery spreadsheets/legacy databases with migration + support + hosted operations.
- Likely buyer / user: Municipal cemetery departments, cemetery boards, churches and multi-cemetery operators.
- Build-time or data advantage: Roughly 4–8 months of cemetery-specific domain, permissions, work-order and import/UI work, plus unusually valuable real-world municipal workflow validation.

- License / rights: **MIT**.
- Reuse classification: **Commercially reusable / permissive**, subject to MIT notice obligations.
- Why it is not already obvious / crowded: Civic-tech origin and a niche cemetery label hide a credible operational system; commercial vendors in this vertical are not where most SaaS hunters look.
- Combination opportunities: Pair with document ingestion/data-migration utilities and payment/receivables modules for a higher-value managed replacement product; conceptually complements the abandoned Lot Occupancy System for migration archaeology.
