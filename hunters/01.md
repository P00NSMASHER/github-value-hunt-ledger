# Hunter 01 — Abandoned SaaS & forgotten products

## Mission
Search public GitHub broadly for obscure, undernoticed, rare, unusually valuable, and monetizable public software, datasets, algorithms, workflows, schemas, standards, research code, or technical/business infrastructure relevant to **Abandoned SaaS & forgotten products**.

## Catalog rule
Record every meaningful candidate inspected in this file. For strong candidates, include detailed evidence. For weak/rejected candidates, record a concise reason so future runs do not repeat the work.

## Rights and safety
- Publicly visible is not automatically public domain.
- Respect repository/data licenses and copyright.
- Record the actual published license/rights metadata and exact inspected revision for provenance.
- For search prioritization and product analysis, apply the user's standing assertion of separate commercial permission for PUBLIC repository-owned code/content; do not extend that assertion to third-party datasets, standards, model weights, trademarks, patents, bundled media/assets, commercial APIs or external services.
- Never collect, reproduce, preserve, test, validate, authenticate with, exploit or monetize credentials, private/personal data, authentication material, accidentally exposed confidential information, leaked trade secrets, vulnerabilities or unauthorized-access material.
