## Verified invariants
The persistence layer now encodes:
1. issued recovery claims and independently observed settlement/counter events as immutable rows;
2. unique external IDs and source hashes;
3. integer-cents money only;
4. append-only amount-bearing allocation edges;
5. one-use gross settlement-event capacity that does not reopen after reversal;
6. net claim capacity that does reopen after a valid return/reversal;
7. a persistent review-only flag once a claim enters partial/split adjudication;
8. exact/unique automatic allocation only when reference, payer, payee, currency, timing, and residual amount all agree;
9. explicit reviewed allocation for partial/split/overpayment cases;
10. append-only reversal edges rather than deletion of the original recovery event;
11. fail-closed partial return handling when one returned amount could map to more than one live allocation edge;
12. SQL triggers for immutability, currency consistency, claim/event capacity, and reversal capacity;
13. `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` write transactions plus lock retry so residual checks occur under a single-writer SQLite boundary.

## Red-team limits
This is a reference SQLite acceptance layer, not customer outcome proof and not yet a multi-tenant production settlement service.

Important remaining limits:
- SQLite `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` deliberately serializes writers; production Postgres or another DB must reproduce these invariants with real transactional/locking semantics rather than assuming the SQLite result transfers automatically.
- automatic candidate uniqueness is enforced in the transaction/service path; the SQL triggers independently protect money capacity/immutability but do not themselves recompute the entire exact-candidate query for a hostile direct `AUTO` insert.
- timestamp fields currently rely on canonical comparable timestamp strings supplied by the ingestion boundary; production ingestion should normalize/validate event time explicitly.
- source identity remains only as trustworthy as the authorized buyer's 820/bank/refund connector. EXP-001 still requires a real frozen buyer population and later external settlement evidence.
- tenant/business-unit isolation, hostile input/parser isolation, and reproducible signed release provenance remain separate registered gaps.

## FAILURES / NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE
- Generic freight/TMS/EDI/reconciliation hunting remains frozen; it would violate the registered stop rule and has lower expected value than obtaining EXP-001 customer evidence.
- Deterministic matching without transactional residual protection is insufficient under concurrency.
- Reversal must reopen claim capacity without reopening the original settlement event's gross capacity.
- Once a claim requires reviewed split/partial allocation, a later numerically exact residual must not silently convert the case back into AUTO.
- Passing an in-memory corpus is not enough; money invariants need persistence-level and race-level tests.
