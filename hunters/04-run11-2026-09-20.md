**IMPLEMENTED — SC Work Order / Purchase Order authority join**
- `SCBill._sync_work_order_context()` reloads the referenced `SC Work Order`, requires `docstatus == 1`, and replaces project, supplier, billing type, BOQ, company and currency from the authoritative work order.
- If a Purchase Order is supplied, `_sync_purchase_order_context()` requires a submitted PO, requires an SC Work Order link, rejects a PO belonging to a different SC Work Order, validates the PO for SC billing, then reloads the commercial context from that source.

**IMPLEMENTED — server-reloaded measured quantity/rate ceiling**
- In measured billing, `_sync_measured_items()` reloads the referenced SC Work Order item rather than trusting submitted rate/assigned quantity fields.

- The Purchase-Order path performs the same pattern against the authoritative PO item and preserves `purchase_order_item`, `sc_work_order_item` and `boq_item` lineage.

**IMPLEMENTED — approval gate into accounting document**
- `approve()` requires a submitted SC Bill and `Submitted` status before setting `Approved`.
- `create_purchase_invoice()` refuses any SC Bill not in `Approved`, refuses a second invoice, then creates a draft Purchase Invoice.
- For measured bills, invoice lines are built from the bill’s server-normalized current quantity and rate and carry project, PO, PO item, SC work-order item, SC-bill item and BOQ-item identifiers.
- The code independently recomputes invoice-line total and refuses to proceed if it differs from SC Bill gross amount.

**IMPLEMENTED — retention/payment authority checks**
- The Payment Entry override validates project/company alignment and payment-account ownership.
- Retention receipts/releases require the correct party type, party, company, payment direction, retention account and positive amount; releases are capped against remaining retention balance.

**SCHEMA VERIFIED**
- `SC Bill` stores explicit SC Work Order, optional Purchase Order, project, supplier, billing type, BOQ, company, currency, bill number, billing period dates, contract value, previous/cumulative billed values, retention, LD deduction, net payable and generated Purchase Invoice.

### RED-TEAM / VERIFIER verdict
**ACCEPT STRONG COMPONENT, NOT MASTER.**

The code closes a real authority join that weaker systems miss, but four boundaries prevent promotion:

1. **No independent field-measurement approval object.** `SC Bill Item.current_qty` is user-entered on the bill and then bounded against work-order/PO quantity. The schema contains no Measurement Book ID, measurement status, inspector/approver identity or measurement date. This is weaker than Nirman’s APPROVED-measurement-derived bill path.

4. **Bank finality is inherited, not construction-native.** Purchase Invoice / Payment Entry ultimately depend on ERPNext bank reconciliation, and current ERPNext history contains a concrete reconciled-vs-clearance inconsistency described below.

# IMPORTANT NEW NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE

## ERPNext false-finality edge case — reconciled Bank Transaction while Payment Entry remains uncleared
- Repository: `frappe/erpnext`
- Current develop revision inspected: `db6e0891099ab27f571b7b9697ba90f6573430f5`
- Issue: `#58323` — “Payment Entry with deducted advance tax remains uncleared after bank reconciliation”
- Issue state at inspection: **OPEN**; created 2026-08-20.

### Verified behavior from the public issue
A Payment Entry with gross USD 500 and a USD 10 deducted advance-tax row produces a net bank movement of USD 490. A USD 490 Bank Transaction can be fully allocated and marked `Reconciled` with zero unallocated amount, while the Payment Entry `clearance_date` remains empty.

The issue’s technical analysis identifies the cause: related bank GL rows are aggregated as `sum(abs(row))`, yielding 510, instead of `abs(sum(signed rows))`, yielding the actual 490 net movement. A proposed fix exists in an external branch/commit, but the upstream issue remains open at this run.

### Why this matters to ScopeSignal
This is a concrete proof that **“bank transaction reconciled” and “voucher/payment cleared” are distinct states and can diverge even in a mature accounting system**. Therefore ScopeSignal must never collapse either field into a generic `PAID` or `CLEARED` state.

### New required EXP-005 adversarial fixture
`RECONCILED_BANK_TRANSACTION__VOUCHER_UNCLEARED_NET_WITHHOLDING`
- invoice/payment nominal amount = 500
- withholding/deducted row = 10
- observed bank movement = 490
- bank transaction allocation = 490 / 490 and reconciliation status = Reconciled
- payment voucher clearance date = null
- expected ScopeSignal result = `SETTLEMENT_STATE_CONFLICT` / REVIEW, not paid-final.

Also add the inverse family: voucher clearance exists but bank-source provenance/freshness is unknown; later return/reversal can still reopen finality.
