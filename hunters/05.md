## Catalog rule
Record every meaningful candidate inspected in this file. For strong candidates, include detailed evidence. For weak/rejected candidates, record a concise reason so future runs do not repeat the work.

- Do not collect, reproduce, preserve, or exploit credentials, private/personal data, authentication material, accidentally exposed confidential information, or leaked trade secrets.

- Why it is not already obvious / crowded: Its name presents it as an MCP server, while the commercially valuable part is a tested cross-source procurement-research and verification layer with edge-case handling that would otherwise be rebuilt inside a SaaS.
- Combination opportunities: Pair with MindPetal/sam-search for opportunity ingestion, zerox for solicitation documents, dedupe for vendor/entity resolution and RulesEngine for evidence-linked bid/no-bid gates.
