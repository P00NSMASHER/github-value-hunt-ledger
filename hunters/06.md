- Build-time or data advantage: High as a clean-room reference for NYC permit/property entity resolution and scheduled enrichment. It demonstrates how to turn DOB records into stable property entities instead of a raw permit feed.

- Combination opportunities: PermitPlate can clean-room the permit→property normalization pattern, then combine it with existing entity-resolution/OCR/rules findings to score trade-specific opportunities without copying the unlicensed implementation.

- Likely buyer / user: Specialty contractors, construction suppliers/manufacturers, distributors, commercial real-estate teams, lenders, site-selection teams and local-market intelligence providers.
- Build-time or data advantage: Extremely high. The city/platform registry and existing source-specific ingestion research could compress months of municipal-source discovery and connector work. The permit-momentum math also gives PermitPlate a higher-value aggregate decision layer beyond individual lead rows.
- Concrete evidence inspected: Exact current commit and recent `US-441` permit commit; root `README.md` listing registered metros/source platforms; recursive tree; `apps/api/src/features/bay_area_permit_momentum.py` implementing `permit_velocity_60_180`, 60-day half-life CapEx density and residential-unit delta; `apps/api/tests/unit/test_bay_area_permit_momentum.py` verifying formulas/windowing/cell isolation; code search confirming `DOBPermitsProducer` and unified `normalize_permit_type`; root `LICENSE`.
- License / rights: Apache-2.0 repository license verified. Each underlying municipal dataset/API still has independent terms, rate limits and attribution requirements; reuse of connector code does not automatically grant rights to redistribute source data.
- Reuse classification: Directly reusable under Apache-2.0 for repository code, with source-by-source public-data rights review.
- Why it is not already obvious / crowded: It presents itself as a real-estate forecasting engine, so the commercially useful hidden asset for this hunt—the 101-metro municipal ingestion/normalization system and tested permit-feature layer—is easy to miss.
- Combination opportunities: Use Urban Signal as PermitPlate's multi-city source/normalization layer, `dedupe` for entity resolution, official parcel/property adapters for project grouping, then layer trade-specific rules that convert permit type/value/unit-change into contractor/supplier opportunities. This could turn PermitPlate from NYC-centric lead finding into national construction-demand intelligence.
- Next action: Build a source-rights matrix for the highest-value permit feeds and benchmark 3–5 cities end to end into one canonical PermitPlate schema; do not rely on or market Urban Signal's appreciation-prediction claims until independently validated.
