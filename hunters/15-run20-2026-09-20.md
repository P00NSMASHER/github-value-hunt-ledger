**Why unusual / CAP-007 delta**
This is an independent C++ implementation of a pattern already attractive for proof obligations: authority must be authenticated, time-bounded, versioned and internally consistent, while stale or altered authority must fail closed. That gives CAP-007 language/runtime diversity instead of relying primarily on Python/Go-style evidence infrastructure.

A useful transfer contract is:
1. **ROOT / authority registry** — which keys/roles are allowed to authorize proof obligations and evidence catalogs;
2. **OBLIGATION / subject-specific targets** — what decision, subject, control, time period and admissible evidence are required;
3. **EVIDENCE CATALOG / artifact identity** — immutable hash/length/content identity for the evidence object;
4. **SNAPSHOT / consistency set** — exact versions of all policy/evidence metadata that must be considered together;
5. **TIMESTAMP / freshness** — a short-lived freshness statement so an old-but-valid proof set cannot be replayed indefinitely;
6. **LOCAL HIGHEST-SEEN STATE** — reject lower versions/rollback rather than accepting cryptographically valid but obsolete authority.

This does **not** solve whether a policy is legally or operationally correct. It makes policy/evidence provenance, freshness, consistency and rollback behavior auditable and falsifiable.
