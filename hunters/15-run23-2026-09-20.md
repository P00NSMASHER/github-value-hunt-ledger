## FAILURES / NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE
- Historical green E2E is not automatically current qualification for a different subject revision.
- “CI failed” is too coarse for assurance. A harness/bootstrap failure before subject execution is not evidence that the subject failed.
- Unit/lint/fuzz success does not substitute for the real restore drill.
- Conversely, a harness failure must remain non-green for buyer-facing `PROVEN`; absence of current E2E evidence cannot be silently inherited from an older SHA.
- Qualification receipts should include at minimum `{subject_sha, verifier_sha, workflow_sha, test_suite_sha, harness/tool versions, run_id, started_at, completed_at, stage_reached, semantic_result}`.
