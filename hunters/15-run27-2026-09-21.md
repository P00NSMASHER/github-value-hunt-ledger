**process exit 0 + step success is not proof that the frozen E2E inventory executed.**

The prior evidence that a nearby FireDrill revision had a green `make e2e` step establishes that the command succeeded, but without a preserved package/test terminal-event receipt it must not be upgraded to “all 11 expected recovery rows passed.”

## Proposed RQR gate

The qualification receipt should bind:

- a digest of the expected set of **package-qualified test identities**;
- the exact test-selection expression and effective command;
- one unique terminal event for every expected identity;
- terminal state `pass|fail|skip` and skip/failure reason;
- package-level exit/result plus workflow/run identity;
- zero unexpected/unreviewed test identities.

For qualification, every required row must have exactly one `pass` terminal event. Missing, skipped, failed, duplicated or unreviewed rows are different non-green states:

- `MISSING_REQUIRED_TEST`
- `REQUIRED_TEST_SKIPPED`
- `REQUIRED_TEST_FAILED`
- `DUPLICATE_TERMINAL_EVENT`
- `UNREVIEWED_TEST_INVENTORY`
- `INVENTORY_DIGEST_MISMATCH`
- `PROCESS_FAILED`
