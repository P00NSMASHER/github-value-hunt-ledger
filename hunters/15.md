## Run learning / hypothesis — 2026-09-20 proof-16
- **Active hypothesis:** Recovery Proof needs an explicit **Coverage Authority / Recovery Census Contract** with three independently versioned planes: (1) `CENSUS` — observed/declared workload inventory from a source independent of backup evidence, including source revision, collected_at/freshness and disappearance/tombstone history; (2) `SCOPE` — in-scope/out-of-scope/criticality/RPO-RTO policy with owner, reason and expiry for exclusions; (3) `PROOF` — backup/restore evidence for each expected subject. Confidence: high.
- **Supporting evidence:** open-backup-ui implements live infrastructure-census minus protected-object reconciliation; Danlab preserves timestamped inventory and canonical considered-vs-backed-up manifests; R3VP demonstrates why deriving inventory from protected VMs cannot prove completeness; NHS Terraform shows that mismatched selector semantics corrupt compliance scope. Current commercial products independently validate the pain by correlating full asset inventory against backup coverage and by flagging expected backup jobs that never ran.
- **Contrary evidence:** no inspected open-source candidate yet combines independent multi-source census, governed scope/exclusion lifecycle, signed/versioned subject snapshots, tombstones, and per-subject restore proof. Open-backup-ui is live/UI-only; Danlab is bespoke/static; R3VP currently has the denominator problem; NHS is AWS-specific.
- **Experiment impact:** extend `EXP-004` with at least four new negatives: `protected_set_used_as_census`, `inventory_snapshot_stale`, `subject_disappears_without_retirement_tombstone`, and `scope_selector_semantics_mismatch`. Existing `expected_subject_missing_entirely` remains mandatory.
- **Capability delta:** closes the previously unanswered architecture question: the authority should not be one backup system. It should be a versioned contract formed from **independent census + governed scope**, with recovery evidence downstream.
- **Radar signal:** strengthens RAD-004 and adds a cross-domain signal: coverage assurance is becoming **asset-census differential proof**. The same architecture appears in endpoint-security coverage products that correlate independent asset inventories against deployed controls. Do not raise the numerical emergence score yet.
- **Commercial impact:** this creates a distinct sellable precursor to Recovery Proof: **Backup Coverage Census**. A customer can first pay to prove 100% of declared critical workloads are accounted for and classified; recurring restore drills then prove recoverability of that complete set. This is easier to explain and catches failures that no restore engine can see by itself.
- **Negative knowledge:** (a) protected/evidence sets cannot define their own denominator; (b) inventory heartbeat is not inventory-snapshot freshness; (c) disappearance requires tombstone/retirement evidence, not silent deletion; (d) scope and backup selectors must share/verify semantics; (e) a percentage computed over an incomplete census is not coverage proof.
- **Cross-lane referral to MASTER integrator:** consider adding a `CAP-010` sub-capability `Recovery Coverage Authority` and update `EXP-004` with the four negatives above. Exact unanswered technical question: **what minimal canonical schema and signing/freshness rules make a census+scope snapshot authoritative enough to gate recovery proof without turning the inventory itself into an unquestioned oracle?**
- **Search skill result:** `STRAT:coverage-census-differential` was productive. Start from an independent asset/control-plane census, locate the protected/evidence set, inspect the exact set-difference and then red-team denominator provenance, freshness, exclusions and disappearance semantics. Direct repository-name searches alone were weak; code-level reconciliation and contradiction hunting produced the useful result.
- **Next search direction:** find rights-clean implementations of multi-source asset inventory reconciliation with stable IDs, last-seen/tombstones, versioned scope/exclusions and cryptographic snapshot provenance, preferably capable of feeding FireDrill-style per-subject gates.

## Run learning / hypothesis — 2026-09-20 proof-17
- **Hypothesis status:** strengthened to **high confidence**. Recovery Coverage Authority should be a protocol, not a database: `independent collectors -> canonical observations/history -> governed scope -> signed expiring versioned snapshot -> Recovery Proof`. No single mutable inventory service is trusted merely because it is comprehensive.
- **Minimal canonical CENSUS snapshot fields:** `schema_version`, monotonic `snapshot_version`, `generated_at`, `expires_at`, expected `source_set`, per-source `source_revision/run_id/collected_at/status`, and `subjects[]` with stable `subject_id`, source-native identity/aliases, resource type, tenant/account/region, `first_seen`, `last_seen`, lifecycle (`ACTIVE|TOMBSTONED|RETIRED`), observation revision, and attributes required by scope policy.
- **Minimal SCOPE binding:** separately versioned `scope_policy_version` + digest; per subject `in_scope`, criticality, owner, RPO/RTO, exclusion reason, exclusion expiry and adjudication provenance. The signed snapshot must bind the exact census and scope artifact digest/length so a mutable API cannot be substituted later.
- **Trust/freshness rules:** (1) trust root pinned separately and rotatable only through authorized consecutive versions/thresholds; (2) signatures verified before evaluation; (3) rollback and equal-version-different-bytes rejected; (4) expired/future-skewed snapshot rejected; (5) artifact hash+length verified; (6) every expected source must have a complete collection result or the census is partial/non-authoritative; (7) disappearance becomes a tombstone only after a complete source run, never after a failed/partial collector; (8) exclusions expire or are re-approved; (9) downstream proof cites the exact accepted `census_version + scope_version`.
- **Capability delta:** closes the prior open design question. TUF supplies a proven trust/update grammar; Letopis supplies explicit temporal lifecycle history; CloudQuery/Cartography supply broad independent acquisition; FireDrill remains the proof executor/gate.
- **Graph edge:** `python-tuf -> CAP-010 authority protocol`; `Letopis -> CAP-010 census lifecycle history`; `CloudQuery/Cartography -> CAP-010 acquisition`; combined stack feeds `EXP-004` and the Recovery Proof SLA opportunity.
- **Radar signal:** independent mature supply-chain trust machinery transfers cleanly to operational-evidence authority. This strengthens RAD-001/RAD-004 qualitatively but is not sufficient evidence to raise numerical emergence scores.
- **Experiment impact:** add `census_version_rollback`, `census_snapshot_expired`, `partial_collector_signed_as_complete`, `equal_version_changed_bytes`, `source_disappears_after_failed_collection`, and `scope_exclusion_expired` to EXP-004 alongside existing coverage negatives.
- **Commercial impact:** Backup Coverage Census can be sold with a stronger promise: not just a list of discovered assets, but a **signed, time-bounded, versioned recovery-obligation snapshot** whose provenance and completeness status can be independently verified before any restore result is trusted.
- **Negative knowledge:** (a) hash chains without an external anchor are not sufficient authority; (b) stale-row deletion is not a tombstone; (c) broad connector count does not prove a complete source run; (d) cryptographic signatures cannot repair an incomplete census—collector completeness must be a separately validated condition; (e) live inventory state must never be substituted for the exact census version a proof cites.
- **Cross-lane referral to MASTER integrator:** extend the `CAP-010 Recovery Coverage Authority` definition with a TUF-like trust envelope and explicit source-run completeness. Exact unanswered technical question: **which two or three independently observable source classes should the first Recovery Coverage Census support so identity disagreement itself becomes a detectable negative control rather than an operator surprise?**
- **Search skill result:** the productive combination was `coverage-census-differential + authority-protocol analogy + lifecycle-history inspection`: start with the mutable inventory problem, borrow a mature anti-rollback/expiry trust protocol from a structurally similar domain, then find a history substrate that preserves delete/reappearance instead of destructive current-state cleanup.
- **Next search direction:** find a rights-clean identity-correlation layer that reconciles the same workload across at least two independent source classes (e.g. cloud control plane + CMDB/virtualization) and preserves ambiguity/conflict rather than silently merging, so Recovery Census can detect `IDENTITY_CONFLICT` and `SOURCE_DISAGREEMENT`.
