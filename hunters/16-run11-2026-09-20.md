## SERVICE-PO / RECEIPT-AUTHORITY FINDING

The biggest cross-source conclusion is that **“receipt authority” is a policy function, not a single document type**.

- SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS01 documents that performed services are recorded against the PO in a Service Entry Sheet (SES), and in classic service acceptance an invoice can be entered only after the entry sheet is accepted.
- SAP also supports service invoicing **without** SES when the PO line is configured for the relevant invoice-verification mode. Therefore absence of SES is not itself an exception without inspecting PO-line configuration.
- SAP correction semantics are not equivalent to generic AP credit memos: current Business Network guidance says correction SES uses negative quantity and, for over-invoiced service-sheet quantities, directs suppliers to use correction SES rather than credit memos; automatic credit-memo generation from correction SES is not supported in the documented auto-invoice flow.
- Business Central uses line-level receive/invoice quantities and requires receipt before invoicing a PO line unless receiving and invoicing occur together.
- ERPNext makes Purchase Receipt requirement configurable and supplier-overridable; current issues show non-stock status and amount-vs-quantity ambiguity.

**Design implication:** EXP-002 should replace a boolean `has_receipt` rule with a `ReceiptAuthorityPolicy` resolved from `{ERP/system, PO line type, buyer configuration, supplier override, verification mode}`. The authority evidence can then be `GoodsReceipt`, `ServiceEntrySheet`, `DirectInvoiceAllowed`, or another configured acceptance artifact. Source-run truth (`PRESENT / VERIFIED_EMPTY / UNAVAILABLE`) remains a separate dimension.

## EMERGING TECHNOLOGY / CATEGORY SIGNAL
**Authority-aware procurement state machines** are becoming the important layer above ordinary three-way matching. The meaningful state is no longer `(PO, receipt, invoice)` as three booleans; it is a typed, policy-resolved evidence graph with line IDs, receipt/acceptance document class, correction lineage, buyer configuration and source-observation status.

Commercial importance: high. Technical maturity: high inside mature ERPs, low in independent audit products. Cross-domain applicability: medium-high (AP, procurement, logistics, service acceptance, construction/progress billing). Adoption acceleration not independently quantified this run.

## FAILURES / NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE
1. Do not key AP authority to header totals; receipt/invoice consumption must remain line-specific.
2. Do not let the same PO-level invoiced pool be consumed by multiple receipts.
3. Keep residual **amount and quantity** synchronized after partial/direct allocations.
4. Do not treat ERP document `status = Completed` as authoritative evidence; amount-based completion can hide un-invoiced quantity.
5. Do not treat `missing receipt` as an error without line type + buyer configuration + ERP verification mode.
6. Do not accept “any error occurred” as a successful negative test. Expected failure must assert exact semantic class/invariant.
7. SAP service corrections may be correction-SES lineage, not ordinary credit-memo lineage. AP recovery fixtures need both.
