### Concrete capability
Odoo implements **counter-event-aware PO-line state**, not only terminal invoice/receipt statuses:

1. **Purchase credit notes release billed quantity on the exact PO line.**
   - `addons/purchase/models/purchase_order_line.py::_prepare_qty_invoiced` walks invoice lines linked through `purchase_line_id`.
   - `in_invoice` quantities are added; `in_refund` quantities are subtracted after UoM conversion.
   - Cancelled invoices are excluded from normal billed quantity.
   - `qty_to_invoice` is recomputed from either ordered quantity or received quantity depending on the line/product purchase method.

2. **Physical returns can reduce the exact PO line's received quantity, but only when policy says the return should update/refund the PO.**
   - `addons/purchase_stock/models/purchase_order_line.py` keeps `stock.move` rows linked by `purchase_line_id`.
   - For completed purchase-return moves, `to_refund=True` decrements that line's received quantity.
   - Return-of-return and dropship edge cases are handled separately to avoid naïve double counting.
   - A return with `to_refund=False` can represent a physical stock movement without changing the commercial received-quantity authority on the PO line.

3. **The behavior is exercised end-to-end in tests.**
   - `addons/purchase_stock/tests/test_purchase_order.py::test_02_po_return` creates a two-line PO, receives and bills five units per line, returns two with `to_refund=True`, verifies receipt quantity falls, posts a vendor refund, then verifies `qty_invoiced` is reduced from five to three on the PO lines.
   - `test_receive_returned_product_without_po_update` performs a return and return-of-return with `to_refund=False`; after the physical round trip, PO received quantity remains unchanged, proving physical movement and purchasing authority are distinct states.
   - `addons/purchase/tests/test_purchase_invoice.py::test_vendor_bill_delivered` cancels a bill and verifies billed quantity falls back to zero and invoiceable quantity reopens.
   - `test_vendor_bill_delivered_return` reduces received quantity after a completed bill, produces a negative `qty_to_invoice`, creates the corrective invoice/refund and verifies the PO settles at the corrected billed quantity.
   - Ordered-quantity and received-quantity products follow different invoiceability policy, preventing one universal receipt rule.

### Why it is unusual / reusable
The valuable element is not Odoo as an ERP. It is the tested separation of **physical return, purchasing receipt authority, billed quantity and corrective financial document** on stable PO-line identity. That gives EXP-002 a real counter-event oracle for cases where a return should restore authority, where it deliberately should not, and where a credit note changes billed state without erasing history.

### Buyer / painful problem / first wedge
- Buyer: controller, AP shared services, recovery/audit provider.
- Pain: recovery engines often treat receipt/invoice state as terminal and can continue to assert leakage after a legitimate return, credit or cancellation—or incorrectly free authority after a purely physical stock movement.
- First paid wedge: closed-period AP Leakage Acceptance Test with explicit return/credit/cancellation replay and zero unsupported recovery dollars.

### Build compression / moat impact
This compresses several weeks of domain-state discovery and, more importantly, supplies independently implemented regression semantics for the ERP-neutral authority graph. The moat is the cross-ERP corpus of exact counter-event invariants, not copying Odoo business objects.

### Red-team findings / strongest objections
1. **UoM rounding is not an exact-money oracle.** Open issue `odoo/odoo#262244` (opened 2026-04-30 against 19.0) reports `qty_invoiced` can be biased +0.001 because `_prepare_qty_invoiced` uses default UP rounding during UoM conversion. Example: seven units against one dozen can produce 0.584 rather than 0.583. EXP-002 must therefore normalize quantity math with an explicit precision/rounding contract rather than treating Odoo's floating quantity as unquestioned truth.
2. **Default bill-line matching is weaker than BCApps ambiguity refusal.** `addons/purchase/models/purchase_bill_line_match.py` can match previously unlinked bill/credit lines to PO lines by product and assign `purchase_line_id`. With multiple same-product PO lines, this is an operational convenience, not independent proof of line ownership. Our authority layer must preserve or require exact source line identity and route ambiguous same-product ownership to REVIEW.
3. **Counter-events are explicit, not magical.** A physical return only changes purchasing receipt state when the `to_refund` semantics say it should, and a vendor credit note remains a separate financial counter-event. This is a strength for modeling but means a product cannot infer financial reversal from stock movement alone.
4. **Odoo does not replace the BCApps authority-consumption graph.** It provides strong reversal semantics; BCApps remains the stronger exact allocation/conservation reference for invoice↔PO↔receipt ownership.

### Commercial implication
EXP-002 can now model a money-bearing edge as a **consumable authority allocation with explicit counter-events**, rather than overwriting current quantities. A credit note releases only the billed capacity it references; a qualifying return reduces only the receipt authority it references; a non-refunding physical return does not automatically reopen commercial authority.

## CLAIMS TESTED

## EXP-002 REGRESSION DELTA
Add the following mandatory cases to the ERP-neutral corpus:
1. **Receive 5 / bill 5 / return 2 with commercial-refund flag / credit 2 -> received 3 and billed 3** on the exact original PO line.
2. **Physical return with no PO/refund update -> inventory changes but commercial receipt authority does not reopen.**
3. **Cancel vendor bill -> billed capacity reopens without deleting the historical bill/cancellation evidence.**
4. **Credit-only correction -> billed capacity changes but physical receipt state does not.**
5. **Two PO lines with the same SKU -> no product-level auto-assignment may establish authority; ambiguous ownership = REVIEW.**
6. **UoM adversary: 1 dozen ordered, 7 each billed, precision 3 -> normalized expected quantity must not drift by +0.001 because of an implicit UP rounding mode.**
7. **Ordered-basis vs received-basis/service policy -> reversal semantics follow the authoritative line policy, not a universal `has_receipt` rule.**
