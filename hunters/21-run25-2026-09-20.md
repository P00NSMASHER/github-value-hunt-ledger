### What is implemented
OpenTFRaw is a Rust/Python Thermo Fisher `.raw` reader. Unlike ThermoRawFileParser, the inspected parser crate does not depend on Thermo's RawFileReader/vendor SDK: `crates/opentfraw/Cargo.toml` depends on `thiserror` and `openmassspec-core`, while `reader.rs` directly performs little-endian binary parsing and dispatches Thermo-format-specific run/scan/index/event/data structures. The crate forbids unsafe Rust.

The project claims support for RAW format versions 8, 47, 57, 60, 62, 63, 64 and 66 and documents coverage from LCQ/LTQ through Orbitrap/Q Exactive/Tribrid/Exploris/Astral and TSQ families. `CORPUS.md` describes a ~124 GB, 283-file PRIDE-derived validation corpus spanning PacketHeader and Flat encodings plus DDA, DIA, MS3, EThcD, UVPD, PRM and SRM cases.
