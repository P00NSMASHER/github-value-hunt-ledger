- Concrete evidence inspected: Root contains `api.py`, ~15 KB `main.py`, `algorithms/`, `optim/`, React `front/`, config/data/research paper; `algorithms/helpers.py` ~13 KB and `algorithms/run.py` ~5.4 KB. `run.py` actually iterates work slots/cycles, filters feasible tanks, handles depot returns/break time and chooses the next stop.
