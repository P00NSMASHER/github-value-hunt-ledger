# Hunter Learning Measurement Curriculum

## Purpose

The adaptive hunter system now has a training environment, outcome-weighted memory, independent confirm evidence, repair/falsification queues, and gated skill promotion. The remaining bottleneck is **evidence density**: live value memory cannot safely steer search until enough independently partitioned observations exist.

The learning curriculum converts that evidence deficit into a deterministic measurement plan without turning measurement into exploitation.

## Non-negotiable boundary

The curriculum is **measurement-only**.

It does not:

- change learned allocator weights;
- reactivate paused hunters;
- itself authorize execution or create a worker claim;
- select a strategy because its Q-value or reward is high;
- use current policy allocation as a selection signal;
- inspect or choose train/confirm membership;
- retry work to obtain a different partition;
- convert manual/legacy/unallocated work into confirm evidence;
- bypass domain STOP gates, claims, verifier gates, or skill-promotion gates.

Its only purpose is to answer:

> Which active search strategies have the most useful evidence deficit to measure next?

## Selection policy

Three measurement reservations are generated by default:

1. **two gate-closing slots** — active strategies nearest to their current evidence gate;
2. **one zero-run exploration slot** — preserves coverage of unmeasured strategies.

The priority formula uses only evidence quantity/progress:

- measured train runs;
- train deep inspections;
- confirm runs;
- confirm deep inspections;
- current gate state.

It does **not** use:

- Q-value;
- mean reward;
- realized commercial reward;
- current policy allocation.

Those fields may be emitted for auditability but cannot affect curriculum selection.

## Phases

### train_measurement

The strategy has not yet cleared the current train evidence gate.

Current gate:

- >=5 eligible train runs;
- >=20 train deep inspections.

### confirm_measurement

The train gate is clear but independent confirmation evidence is incomplete.

Current confirmation gate also preserves the stronger fail-closed rules from the learning engine:

- >=2 confirm runs;
- >=6 confirm deep inspections;
- confirm mean reward >=0;
- no negative confirm episode;
- >=1 positive confirm episode.

### repair_or_falsify

The strategy has an \`overfit_signal\` or \`confirm_regression_signal\`.

It is suppressed from measurement allocation and should enter reproduction/repair analysis instead.

### ready

The strategy already satisfies train + confirm evidence requirements.

It receives no measurement priority.

## Blind partition rule

Every recommendation requires the normal generated assignment/claim path.

Partition membership must not be calculated, exposed, selected, released, retried, or otherwise used to alter execution before canonical ingestion.

A no-find or failed run may only be retried for an independent operational reason. It may never be retried to seek another train/confirm partition.

## Generated artifacts

The shared intelligence build produces:

- \`intelligence/learning_curriculum.json\`;
- \`intelligence/LEARNING_CURRICULUM.md\`.

The top recommendations also appear in \`NETWORK_PRIORS.md\` as measurement debt.

These artifacts remain advisory. The allocator and live policy are unchanged.
