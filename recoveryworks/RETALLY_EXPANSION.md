# RETALLY staged supplier-spend recovery pilots

Status: engineering preparation, not commercial validation. RETALLY is the
customer-facing brand; RecoveryOS remains the shared evidence and ledger platform.
No customer recovery, signed engagement, or profitable delivery is established
by this change. Existing freight launch and authorization controls remain intact.

## Sequence and offer

| Stage | Services | Customer evidence | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP and approved supplier credits | Cleared payment allocations, invoices, dated supplier statements, approved credit entitlement and settlement history | Independently reviewed findings, overlap disposition, customer-authorized recovery, reconciled actual outcome and delivery cost |
| 2 | Procurement and rebates | Effective PO/contract prices, independent received/billable quantities, complete qualifying purchase population, rebate terms and settlements | Same exit evidence; reconcile AP overlap before counting dollars |
| 3 evaluation | Parcel and utilities | Reviewed shipment-specific carrier rates; actual utility, effective tariff versions and billing/usage evidence | Demonstrate support for the customer's actual rate/tariff before offering the service |

Initial customer profile: distributors, wholesalers and manufacturers with an AP
owner able to supply exports and controlling documents. This is a targeting
hypothesis, not demonstrated demand. Start with a bounded period and invoice
population agreed with the customer; record an analyst-hour cap before work.

Proposed positioning: **RETALLY finds and helps recover documented supplier
overpayments and unpaid credits. Find it. Prove it. Recover it.**
Free initial audit; proposed 30% contingency on agreed, attributable net realized
recovery; no recovery/no fee. Define treatment of applied credits, reversals,
existing disputes and incumbent recoveries in the engagement. Future savings,
unpaid invoices avoided and unposted credits are not automatically recovered cash.
Funds remain customer-owned. This document is operating guidance, not a signed
customer agreement or authorization to contact counterparties.

## Create a private intake kit

Run from the repository, writing customer files outside it:

```bash
python -m recoveryworks.expansion_pilot init --directory /private/client-stage1 --client-id client-001 --stage 1
python -m recoveryworks.expansion_pilot check --config /private/client-stage1/scan.json
python -m recoveryworks.expansion_pilot run --config /private/client-stage1/scan.json --state /private/client-ledger.json --report /private/stage1-report.json
```

Use stages `2` and `3` in separate new directories. Reuse the same private ledger
for the same customer and currency. The generator refuses to overwrite an
existing directory. Source templates start blank and verification flags are false.
Set AP/utility fallback effective dates from reviewed evidence; no dates are guessed.
Replace the utility placeholder. Program/tariff JSON starts as an empty list;
use INGESTION_REBATE.md and INGESTION_AP_UTILITY.md for supported authority formats.

The kit intentionally requests complete sources for its paired services. If only
one service is in scope, remove the other branch from scan.json. These kits are
stricter than the generic Scan 360 runner: every listed source needs records.
Never invent a zero settlement to satisfy this requirement. Document genuine zero
settlement evidence or stop that service. Never set a verification flag solely
because a file was received, hashed, parsed or generated by a test.

`check` verifies basic intake shape and population only. It does not verify
customer ownership, authenticity, completeness, entitlement or deadlines. `run`
uses existing adapters and private ledger persistence; all existing approval and
customer-authorization gates still apply. Static pilot_status.json is an intake
label, not a live readiness report. Scan reports explicitly retain a pending
commercial-validation and cross-branch overlap warning.

## Review and prevent double counting

Before quoting any combined recovery, populate overlap_review.csv for every
candidate with a reviewer-assigned Economic_Loss_ID and source reference.
Group the same underlying loss across AP, freight, credits, procurement and rebates.
Record INCLUDE, SUPPRESS or HOLD, the related finding and a reason. An invoice
can contain several distinct losses, so neither invoice equality nor amount
equality is sufficient to decide overlap. Do not simply add branch totals.

Examples: a missed rebate appearing as a supplier statement credit is one loss;
an unpaid credit for a procurement price adjustment is one loss. A separate
duplicate cash payment can be a distinct loss but needs reconciled payment and
credit evidence. Check refunds, offsets, reversals, rebills and already-known
matters before inclusion. Unresolved groups stay HOLD. The new tool flags this
manual control; it does not implement automatic cross-branch deduplication.

## Complete a real pilot

1. Identify the customer entity, sponsor and data owner. Obtain the signed scope,
   data-processing/retention terms and approved private intake route required by
   existing customer onboarding. Freeze the population and known matters.
2. Reconcile exports to control totals; retain original sources, hashes, effective
   contracts and verification notes. Normalize copies into the intake templates.
3. Run the selected stage. Investigate every exception and independently recalculate
   proposed findings, including legitimate/no-recovery examples.
4. Complete overlap review. Obtain customer authorization for each exact outbound
   recovery action using the existing ledger controls. Detection is not permission.
5. Reconcile actual customer cash or agreed applied credits, including reversals,
   to original findings. Record collected fees separately from expected fees.
6. Populate economics.csv with actual delivery minutes and costs. Contribution is
   actual fee less labor and other attributable costs; report elapsed recovery time,
   reviewed false positives and customer effort too. Uncollected fees are not cash.
7. Review evidence and economics before launching the service publicly. A passing
   software test, candidate amount or customer acceptance alone is insufficient.

No fixed recovery percentage or turnaround is promised. Parcel remains dependent
on a reviewed rate assessment; it is not a complete automated carrier-rating or
late-delivery refund service. Utilities support the documented deterministic tariff
primitives; unsupported formulas and periods spanning tariff changes must remain
exceptions. Broader parcel/utility launch is deferred until those customer-specific
inputs and operations are demonstrated.
