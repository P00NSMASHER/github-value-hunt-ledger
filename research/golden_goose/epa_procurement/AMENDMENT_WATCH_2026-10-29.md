## Implemented

- `mandate_guard.py` screens exact project scope, official-notice receipt structure, publication-versus-observation timestamps, pending-versus-final amendment claims, stale bids and deadline gaps. Outputs only `REVIEW` or `NOT_ACTIONABLE`, never asserted financial opportunity.
- `akron_2026_amendment.json` preserves the proposed-status official-source case and explicitly states that $300m is *not recoverable revenue*.
- `test_mandate_guard.py` includes **13 unit methods** and **20 separate adversarial subcases**. All passed in a locally constructed Python copy on Oct. 29; **GitHub CI / independently reproduced commit remains unverified**.
- Reproduce: from this directory, run `python -m unittest test_mandate_guard -v`.

**Residual authority risk:** The offline gate cannot authenticate bytes at a claimed government URL; a fabricated but government-shaped notice ID may still pass the *syntax* check. It nevertheless yields REVIEW, not a green lead. Source existence, exact project mapping, later amendments, court order entry and current tender status must be independently verified before sales use. The library does not give legal advice.
