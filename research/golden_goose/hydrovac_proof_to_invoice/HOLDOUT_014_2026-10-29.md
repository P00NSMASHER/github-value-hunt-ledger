## Frozen adversarial test and independently observed failure
Authored eleven entirely synthetic safety test methods **before** changing the existing #870 reconciler. Pre-patch, **7/11 failed**:
1. Mixed ISSUED+DRAFT invoice lines were mistakenly treated as absent billable hours or dump fees, creating fictional **$175** and **$180** monetary candidates even though those components were present in a draft line.
2. Two rows sharing the same job ID but distinct field-ticket IDs produced **two $880** fictional candidates (**$1,760 gross** for a potentially duplicated $880 job); case/whitespace variants also evaded the prior ticket-only de-duplication.
3. An omitted `invoices` ledger key was wrongly treated as an explicitly exported empty ledger and produced a candidate.
4. Case/whitespace variants of the same invoice line within one job bypassed duplicate-line review.
5. An invoice dated before service was misinterpreted as authoritative post-work reconciliation; advance-billing may be legitimate but requires human review.

This is a **selected, purpose-built defect-seeking set**, not random-sample model accuracy, actual overbilling or recoverable dollars. In particular, synthetic $1,760, $175 and $180 are *not collectible*.
