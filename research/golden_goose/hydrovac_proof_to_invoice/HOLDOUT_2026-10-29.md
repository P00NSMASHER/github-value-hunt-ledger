## Out-of-sample safety holdout

A separately authored, synthetic **12-test** holdout tested the existing #870 Python engine **before modifying it**. **8 of 12 tests failed**, exposing monetary **false-positive candidate** conditions. Specifically:
- Two distinct job IDs referencing **one** field-ticket ID could generate **$1,760 synthetic gross potential** for a single **$880** fictional job; this is neither true receivable nor money collectible. The post-fix output is **0 monetary candidates and 2 review rows** until independent reconciliation.
- One invoice-line identifier reused across jobs was not detected at the cohort level.
- A more recent signed rate card or credited/voided invoice record did not prevent the older rate/invoice from becoming a positive monetary candidate.
- Case/whitespace-normalized duplicate ticket identifiers and malformed explicit ticket IDs were not fully controlled.

Fixed **in the existing draft PR #870**, without touching protected main: optional ticket-ID normalization and cohort-wide ticket/line collision review, unresolved rate history to REVIEW, credit memo/reversal/void metadata to REVIEW, and malformed supplied ticket ID to REVIEW. **12/12 holdout tests then passed locally**, including genuine split-invoice aggregation and waiver controls.
