# Golden Goose 016: signed Michigan hydrovac contract defeats naive cash-gap detection

Date: October 29, 2026. Research only; no actual buyer data, revenue, authorization or collection.

**Official contract:** https://www.michigan.gov/dtmb/-/media/Project/Websites/dtmb/Procurement/Contracts/021/250000000902.pdf . State of Michigan/MDOT contract 250000000902 with Total Venture LLC dba Brothers Hydrovac, effective June 26, 2025 to Jan 1, 2028. Inspect PDF pages 1, 9, 29: (a) **1% invoice discount if paid within 30 days of receipt, otherwise net 45**; (b) disputed amounts/setoffs and final-payment waivers must be examined; (c) proper disposal documentation plus itemized invoice/delivery order required; (d) change notices can supersede pricing. This public document is an example of real contractual complexity, NOT proof of this vendor's actual invoices, paid cash, missed collections or an offer of engagement.

**Material falsifier:** A fictional $10,000 invoice paid in full at $9,900 on day 29 is a *correctly discounted payment*, not a $100 collectible loss. The same fictional $9,900 cash on day 31 produces a $100 apparent discrepancy only after many proof requirements, but **still not a collectible debt** absent legal/contract authority. The published **$1,293,128 estimated contract value** belongs to an unrelated third-party state agreement and does NOT represent our sales, an available fund, contractor earnings, or a recoverable amount.
