### Evidence / exact scope
- Existing Python experiment `hydrovac_reconcile.py`, remote blob **74082763b845c0f7a9fd3549790e04ae4db64fe4**, classifies some rows `CLEAR_RECONCILED` by summing **hours_qty** and **dump_qty** and comparing authorized quantities. The inspected `inspect` function does **not** compare posted line unit rates or monetary extensions against the rate card. A clean *quantity* result therefore cannot prove a correct posted *dollar* amount. This is a source-level finding, not a successfully reproduced live customer error.
- Federal Acquisition Regulation **32.905(b)(1)(iv)** lists description, quantity, unit of measure, unit price and extended price as elements of a proper **federal** invoice: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/subpart-32.9 . **Scope warning:** this general federal process example does not establish governing terms for the separately examined Michigan state contract or Worcester municipal bid.
- Existing source-bound public purchasers: executed Michigan MDOT contract [250000000902](https://www.michigan.gov/dtmb/-/media/Project/Websites/dtmb/Procurement/Contracts/021/250000000902.pdf); Worcester published [bid and award listing CR-8693-J6](https://www.worcesterma.gov/finance/purchasing-bids/bids/cr-8693-j6). Those prices and a contract's estimated value are **not** any customer's invoices or our revenue.
- Overlapping substitutes already marketed: [Aimsio](https://aimsio.com/industries/hydrovac/) integrates signed tickets, rate books and invoices; [Spira](https://www.spiradata.com/hydrovac-software) markets field-ticket-to-invoice workflows. Their own descriptions do not establish that a buyer would pay for an additional audit.

### Adversarial numerical example
A **fictional** 4-hour/one-load job at a **fictional** signed $175/hour + $180/load equals **$880**. If a posted invoice carries the same 4-hour/one-load quantities but uses **$160/hour**, its illustrative total is **$820**, a **$60 arithmetic difference**. Quantity-only matching cannot detect that dollar mismatch. It is **not** evidence that any real customer is entitled to $60 or that we could collect it. Likewise, a document with a hidden $100 discount or a changed rate could be correct; amounts cannot be interpreted without complete signed amendments, credits, discounts and final-payment evidence.

### Tangible build and reproducibility
On existing **draft PR #870** only, added `posted_amount_guard_018.js`: a sidecar **synthetic-only** checker that requires exact posted line identity, signed-rate flags, complete billed hours/loads, unit rates, extended prices, cent-exact line and ledger totals, reviewed discounts/credits/waivers and strict fixed schemas. On uncertainty, `REVIEW`; the only non-review output is `SYNTHETIC_ARITHMETIC_ONLY`. Both responses always set collectible to null, owner revenue to zero and real customer invoices reviewed to zero.

Tested via `new Function(<exact returned JS source> + "\\nreturn runHoldout();")()` in the local V8 tool runtime. After source readback, **25/25** original adversarial cases passed. An additional independent attack adding an unmodeled `discount_usd: '100.00'` to an otherwise correct posted line **incorrectly returned `SYNTHETIC_ARITHMETIC_ONLY`**. Hardened both job-level and invoice-line allowlists to fail closed for unfamiliar fields, added three regressions, and obtained **28/28** in-process cases: **25 REVIEW, 3 arithmetic-only, 0 revenue or collectible assertions**. Exact GitHub-readback JS blob `288f4281aba8d8a901362bcd1588a9da5143ecaf` was independently reexecuted with **28/28** tests passing. GitHub CI release-gate completion is still unverified here; Node CLI and full Python suite were not executed locally.

This sidecar **does not yet alter or automatically wrap the existing Python classifier**. Treat legacy `CLEAR_RECONCILED` as quantity-only, not complete invoice evidence. External data, accounting system behavior, rate authenticity and customer entitlement remain unvalidated.
