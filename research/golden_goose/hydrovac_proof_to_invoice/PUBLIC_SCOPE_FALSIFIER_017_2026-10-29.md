## Hypothesis and falsification
**Test:** Can a generic “$180 dump fee per completed hydrovac job” be treated as missing collectible revenue? **No**, not from these two official examples. Both make general disposal costs included, not a separate automatic billable item. The old $180/dump in the 100-job demonstration is explicitly fictional and **must not be transferred** to these actual contracts.

- On *one fictional* Michigan $68 regular cleaning, adding $180 disposal produces an unsupported $248 total. **$180 is a counterfactual surcharge**, not a proved invoice gap.
- On *one fictional* Worcester Phoenix one-hour standard call, $315 times one hour overlooks the published four-hour minimum; $1,260 versus $315 is a **$945 public-bid arithmetic difference**, not proof of an undercharge, a signed term, or cash recoverable by anyone. Travel for disposal is within the hourly work day, and no standalone disposal charge is specified.
- Both examples show a **scope collision**: fixed per-basin all-inclusive versus four-hour-minimum hourly pricing. Applying either rate scheme to the other is demonstrably wrong. This is *not* an out-of-sample prediction of billing errors.
