## Smallest first-customer operating protocol
**Gate A: non-sensitive introductory discussion.** Verify genuine contact route, company and sponsor, freight mode and rough scope, incumbent exclusions and feasibility. No confidential attachments. If the contact route is unverified, do not treat a web form event as received or promise an SLA.

**Gate B: authorized 20-invoice-or-smaller feasibility population, only where appropriate.** Independently approve pricing if recovery work is contemplated, staff allocation and two-layer cost cap; freeze buyer-approved scope and record selection, source authority, incumbent and prior credits; verify actual buyer-specific secure access and written confidentiality controls **before** accessing invoices.

**Gate C: independent financial result.** Mark unresolved evidence as REVIEW, record an actual no-recovery outcome as $0 when applicable, give the buyer a traceable calculation and explicit per-claim approval route, and count cash/credits as received only when posted. No carrier contact without a specific written mandate.

**Gate D: close out.** Reconcile settlement, reversals, attributable exclusions and any lawful fee only under actual terms; return/delete customer records per verified retention rules. Case-study permission is optional, never assumed.
