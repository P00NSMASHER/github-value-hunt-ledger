# RETALLY | Minimum-viable first-three-pilot commercial sequence
Version: M2C v1.0 • Internal process. No customer contact authorized here.

## Workflow A — initial inquiry
1. Receive an inquiry through a verified actual route. An email compose window is NOT an inquiry receipt. Do not promise response-time SLAs until confirmed.
2. Log prospect company, contact authority, general freight mode, review periods and approximate invoice availability; do not accept contracts/invoices in ordinary email.
3. Verify fit and screen out legally ineligible/overlapping pre-existing disputes. Record customer intent and boundary conditions.
4. Provide the approved Buyer One-Pager and, when useful, a clearly labeled Sample Recovery Report. These are process examples, not claimed customer achievements.
5. Agree a short scoping conversation and note whether operational and privacy controls are verified before any real document exchange.
**Exit gate:** named decision maker, broad scope, verified contact method, no sensitive attachments.

## Workflow B — qualified proposal
1. Obtain customer-approved carriers, review period, mode, paid invoice population, governing agreements, credits/claims already underway and exclusions, without collecting raw confidential records before secure intake approval.
2. Customize Commercial Proposal, scope worksheet and initial review acceptance criteria. Zero-upfront applies only to bounded agreed eligible scope; not unlimited work.
3. Present Pricing and Scope explanation. The example 20% rate is HYPOTHETICAL ONLY; actual contingency terms need business/counsel approval and signatures.
4. Identify customer reviewer and claim-authority delegate. Explain separate written authorization for each outside action.
5. Deliver welcome packet only after the actual secure-transfer process, confidentiality provisions and responsible reviewers are authorized.
**Exit gate:** signed applicable agreements and secure intake where required, approved review population, customer responsibilities and claim authority identified. If blocked, stop; do not ingest sensitive data.

## Workflow C — findings and recovery decision
1. Issue a dated population/coverage summary with detailed evidence register and exact rate source versions, reviewer names and calculations.
2. Distinguish candidate flags, source-supported differences, separately customer-authorized claims, carrier approvals, unique posted settlements, reversals and contractually fee-eligible funds.
3. Record adverse/missing evidence explicitly. Never silently treat unresolved credits or pre-existing claims as fresh opportunity.
4. Give customer a per-claim decision table: ID, evidence links, supported amount, exclusion status, expected next step, claimant authority and signature/time of approval.
5. Only after each authorization and legal review may carrier communication occur, as a separate authorized action.
6. Reconcile unique actually posted receipts and reversals to accounting records; calculate fees only under approved signed terms and validated eligible base.
**Exit gate:** reproducible signed financial record, separate claim approval records, customer review decision and clear unresolved items.

## Operating evidence for pilot customers 1–3
Each pilot gets a unique folder and immutable references, a named human reviewer, a scope exclusion checklist, a customer-approved secure-transfer method, a decision log, a recovery/credit ledger and a customer closure summary. Do NOT insert prospective names or real confidential records into the public sample.

## Performance metrics (only actual observed results)
Number of accepted leads; fit calls; signed qualified engagements; customer IT hours; invoices reviewed; reviewer minutes; supported findings; authorized claims; posted recoveries; reversals; eligible receipts and signed fee cash collected. A clean review may generate zero recovery. Never count synthetic sample figures as realized commercial performance.
