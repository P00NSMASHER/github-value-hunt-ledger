# Case closure and retention
Authorized manual engagement procedure; not an automatic deletion instruction.

## Close the case
1. Freeze final report, reconciliation, source manifest and snapshot hash. Assign an owner to each pending carrier/posting question.
2. Reconcile benefits, exclusions, invoices, fee credits and any fee refund. Customer recovery funds remain with the customer.
3. Record customer receipt and independent reviewer sign-off; neither proves financial posting.
4. Return agreed records privately, revoke temporary access and verify permissions. Keep legally required billing/contract records under their separate approved rule.
5. Record final-report delivery and the exact retention deadline. Existing manual policy defaults to 30 days after final delivery unless signed terms require a shorter period. An ingestion-based timer must not substitute for this date.

## Holds and deletion
Before deletion, confirm the lawful policy, open claims, required records and holds with the authorized reviewers. Each hold records owner, reason, affected objects and review date.

Inventory originals, derived records, exports, local copies, mail attachments, caches, trash and backups. Request deletion only within approved scope and retain the dated source/hash receipt. Timeout, inaccessible accounts or absent search results mean DELETE_UNKNOWN. Confirm deletion only through actual provider state. Disclose retained backup copies and verified expiry; one folder's deletion is not universal erasure.

Do not purge unresolved inquiries. Production D1 closure/retention migrations await authenticated operators, lawful policy, migration/rollback review and independent approval.

## Interrupted work and incidents
Stop on permission errors, suspicious files, mistaken recipients or unexplained financial differences. Preserve restricted evidence and notify the designated incident owner. Reconcile source receipts, prior actions and current snapshots before resuming. Check uncertain carrier delivery against existing execution evidence before resending.
