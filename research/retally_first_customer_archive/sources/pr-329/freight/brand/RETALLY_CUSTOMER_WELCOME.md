# Welcome to RETALLY
Find it. Prove it. Recover it.

We review freight billing against your records, explain supported differences, and help pursue recoveries you specifically authorize.

## Your first review
We propose a $0-upfront review of up to 20 eligible U.S. domestic USD LTL invoices, subject to fit and reviewer capacity. We agree carriers, dates, records and exclusions before work starts. No recovery amount or turnaround is guaranteed.

1. Identify your authorized signer, company lead, AP contact and transportation/rates contact.
2. Confirm scope, incumbent audit rights, previous findings, disputes and credits.
3. Sign the legally approved engagement and confidentiality terms.
4. Wait for the approved, engagement-specific secure submission invitation.
5. Supply the required records and retain the file/count/hash receipt.
6. Review supported findings and evidence gaps. Approve each outside action separately.
7. Review posted recoveries, reversals, exclusions and contractual fees.

Do not attach confidential records to the public inquiry, chatbot or introductory email. Business contact: jay@retallyrecovery.com. Confidential intake uses the separately verified route.

## Required documents
| Record | Include | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoices | Originals and full line-level export; IDs, currency and totals | Freeze coverage and detect duplicates |
| Rates | Signed contracts, incorporated tariffs, schedules, amendments and effective dates | Establish controlling authority |
| Shipments | BOL/PRO, POD, lane, dates, weight/class and accessorial authorization | Confirm actual service |
| Time charges | Arrival/departure proof, timezone, free time and rounding rules | Validate detention |
| Payments/credits | Paid-invoice ledger, remittances, credit memos and actual posting/application | Distinguish approval from recovery |
| Prior work | Incumbent findings, automatic credits and existing claims | Prevent duplicate claims and fees |
| Authority | Signer, data owner, access roster and approved scope | Protect customer control |

Missing evidence is reported as a gap; it is never replaced with assumed rates or invented payment proof.
